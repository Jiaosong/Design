## Research boundary

This mirror records the stable OLEANDER synthesis of Scandinavian Participatory Design. It does not claim that all co-design, consultation, or user research is participatory design, and it does not replace real participant evidence in projects.

## OLEANDER synthesis — ANALYSIS INFERENCE

`Affected Actors → Participation Rights → Mutual Learning → Making / Prototyping → Conflict Visibility → Decision Impact → Feedback Closure`

This is an OLEANDER synthesis, not a verbatim formula from a single author.

## Transfer rules

1. Define a Participation Contract before a workshop: actors, open questions, locked constraints, influence, decision rights, feedback responsibility.
2. Use manipulable media so practical/tacit knowledge can enter design, rather than asking participants only to evaluate a finished proposal.
3. Preserve conflict; majority preference cannot silently erase minority rights, safety, maintenance, or long-term operations concerns.
4. Participation evidence must enter a Decision Trace: `Actor / Input / Evidence / Conflict / Design Change / Decision / Feedback Status`.
5. Participation never transfers professional responsibility for structure, fire safety, regulation, medical/safety/privacy or other high-risk decisions.

## Non-transferable context

- 1970s–80s Scandinavian trade-union and industrial-democracy conditions are historical context, not a default assumption for every project.
- UTOPIA’s workplace politics and graphic-industry transformation cannot substitute for current stakeholder reality.
- Historical mock-up forms are not mandatory visual language.
- “More participation is always better” is not supported; representation, consent, power asymmetry, cost and responsibility remain constraints.

## AI boundary

AI may structure real participant input, identify omissions/conflicts, maintain Decision Trace and check feedback closure. AI must not invent participants or opinions, treat synthetic personas as participation evidence, smooth away rights conflicts, or claim user validation without real actor evidence.

## Practice gate

`Participation Impact Audit v0.1` = DESIGN-READY FOR FUTURE TEST / NOT RUN.

A future OLEANDER practice may only claim real Participatory Design evidence when it retains:

- Real Actor Evidence
- Session / Making Evidence
- Decision Impact
- Feedback Closure

Failure-seeking cases include consultation disguised as participation, dominant actors suppressing others, missing affected groups, AI smoothing conflict, input producing no design change, missing feedback, and unclear rights for participation materials.
