## Core method
Treat design as a testable rule system rather than a surface style:

1. Define available standard materials, tools, use task, structural and safety boundaries.
2. Reduce the object to a limited set of components, joints, dimensional rules and assembly sequence.
3. Build or simulate the rule and record errors, resistance, material response and action burden.
4. Generate variants without breaking the locked constraints.
5. Observe use and compare intended rules with actual behavior.
6. Revise the rules; preserve failure evidence and assumptions.

Output is not only an object. It includes rules, assembly record, failure record and modifiable parameters.

## Representative evidence
- 16 animali (1957): continuous cutting generates whole/unit/relational structure.
- Glifo (1966): modular unit + joint + reconfiguration system.
- Proposta per un'Autoprogettazione (1973): standard materials and reproducible instructions make construction a way to learn design judgment.
- Eppur si muove (1979): participant behavior becomes part of the evidence.

## Transferable to OLEANDER
- Convert abstract ideas into finite rules + executable actions + observable results.
- Use standardized units to test relation, hierarchy, rhythm, proportion and scale before styling.
- Treat material specification, processing and connection as core design variables.
- Preserve assembly errors and failure modes as evidence.
- Treat instructions, parameter tables and sequence as design outputs.
- AI may help decompose components/constraints, generate variant matrices, check rule conflicts and summarize repeated failure patterns.

## Must not be copied directly
- Do not turn rough timber, nails or exposed construction into a fixed OLEANDER visual language.
- Do not detach the method from its 1970s Italian political, labor and anti-consumer context and present that context as universal.
- User participation does not waive engineering, safety, accessibility, fire, compliance, manufacturing or procurement requirements.
- No historical material or dimension may be promoted to a current project specification without project-level evidence.
