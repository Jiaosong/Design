# 2026-08-12｜IP03｜Automotive Reference Vehicle v0.2｜Comprehensive Modeling Benchmark

**Status:** EXPLORATION → F1 DESIGN VALIDATION

v0.1 is rejected as a representative vehicle because the greenhouse was too tall, the floating roof insert and full-ring wheel-arch trim were visually implausible, the wheel spokes were mis-scaled, and the first QA contract mixed body width with mirror-overall width.

## v0.2 corrections
- lower/narrower greenhouse and roofline;
- flush panoramic roof insert;
- body-width and mirror-overall-width QA separated;
- top-half wheel-arch trim instead of full torus rings;
- open rim ring + hub + thin radial spokes + brake disc/caliper;
- center sill only between wheel arches;
- slimmer integrated head/tail lamps and light guides;
- refined mirrors, door handles, seams and cabin proportions.

## Design target
Generic unbranded fastback/crossover:
- body length 4.42 m
- body width 1.86 m
- exterior height about 1.44 m
- overall width with mirrors about 2.08 m
- wheelbase 2.72 m
- track 1.58 m
- wheel outer radius 345 mm

## Review rule
Machine PASS is insufficient. Visual/Product QA must verify stance, wheel-to-body ratio, overhangs, greenhouse/body ratio, roof and beltline, wheel-arch logic, surfacing continuity, panel-gap integration, light integration, clipping/occlusion and whether the result reads as a coherent vehicle rather than primitive assembly.

## Reality boundary
Designer benchmark only; not engineering CAD, crash, aero, package, homologation or manufacturing validation.
