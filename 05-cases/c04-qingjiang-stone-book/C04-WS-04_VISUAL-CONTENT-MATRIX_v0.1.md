## 6. Failure signs

- UI is stronger than landscape at first glance.
- Mark could be transplanted to any tourist destination.
- inherited dark/bronze palette recolors the real landscape.
- crop forces a figurative/mythic reading before users see the actual object.
- S0 gains cards, long text or competing primary actions.
- S2 diagram cannot be located back in the photograph.
- third-party copyrighted preview becomes indistinguishable from publishable production media.

## 7. Current decision corridor

`real photography dominant + minimal graphic intervention + relation-driven marks + explanation expanded only where needed, primarily S2`.

This is an acceptable corridor for the next experiment, not a final style freeze.
