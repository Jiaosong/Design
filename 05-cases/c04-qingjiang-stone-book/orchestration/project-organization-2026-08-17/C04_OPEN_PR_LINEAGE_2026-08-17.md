## E｜Cleanup discipline

1. Mark older same-object pixels `SUPERSEDED CANDIDATE` only when a later material delta is explicit.
2. Keep unique recoverable design/proof in open PRs until indexed.
3. Do not close/delete PRs merely for repository cleanliness.
4. Do not merge candidates only to simplify lineage.
5. When a candidate is selected, update canonical object/current pointer before downstream Web/F consumption.
6. Always preserve latest independent annotation beside the candidate it judges.
7. Do not let self-reported PR QC override a later independent Design Quality verdict.
