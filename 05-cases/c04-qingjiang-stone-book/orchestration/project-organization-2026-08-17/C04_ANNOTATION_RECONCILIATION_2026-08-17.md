## 0｜Why this overlay exists

PR titles and bodies describe a candidate at creation/update time. Later GitHub issue comments or PR reviews may contain a newer independent design read, supersession note, source correction, or presentation verdict.

This overlay therefore adds a sixth organizational layer:

`DESIGN SOURCE → MERGED CURRENT AUTHORITY → CANDIDATE DELTA → TECHNICAL/VALIDATION PROOF → DOWNSTREAM CARRIER → LATEST INDEPENDENT ANNOTATION / VERDICT`.

Precedence rule:
- annotation/review may **reinterpret the presentation status of a candidate**;
- it may identify a newer Drive pixel/material delta as the strongest candidate;
- it may demote candidate pixels to SUPPORT/PROCESS/REVISE;
- it may not silently promote an open PR to merged CURRENT;
- it may not delete the upstream canonical design object;
- `CONCEPT KEEP ≠ PIXEL KEEP`; `PIXEL FAIL ≠ DESIGN DELETE`.
