## Required independent Design Crit
Judge actual rendered pixels separately from file integrity.

1. First-read recognition: does it read as `清江石书`, not a generic park logo?
2. Qingjiang specificity: does the mark encode a credible river-valley/page relation without literal theme-park illustration?
3. Existing-logo lineage: is continuity visible enough that this is a rebuild, not gratuitous replacement?
4. Composition and proportions: mark/wordmark/endorsement hierarchy.
5. Small-size behavior: 24 / 32 / 48 / 96 px.
6. Monochrome behavior: no reliance on River color.
7. Landscape-yield behavior: identity must retreat in real scenic imagery.
8. Cross-media fit: guide, wayfinding, reading card, digital memory.
9. Cultural restraint: no generic ethnic pattern, faux seal, pseudo-traditional decoration.
10. Professional finish: spacing, curves, stroke optical balance, typography.
11. Truth boundary: brand must not imply field validation, live operation, survey precision or geology approval.
