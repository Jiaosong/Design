## Core rule

Do not map engineering closure to design quality:
- `Artifact existence ≠ Design quality`
- `Traceability ≠ Professional finish`
- `Evidence correctness ≠ Visual excellence`
- `Process PASS ≠ MAIN KEEP`

Default editorial rule:

> **NO COMPRESSION / NO LOSS / RESTRUCTURE WITHOUT INFORMATION LOSS**

The project may become clearer, better ordered, more hierarchical, more visually restrained, or more selective in emphasis. It may not become smaller by silently deleting established layers, design systems, outcomes, or evidence chains.

## Non-negotiable execution constraints

1. Do not reduce the current 12-layer architecture to 9, 8, or fewer layers without an explicit `CUT / DEMOTE` design decision.
2. Do not merge away independently valid `Audience Depth`, `Physical / Sensory`, `Memory / IP`, `Technical Proof`, or `Return` chapters.
3. Do not delete complete design logic because of webpage length, presentation rhythm, visual simplicity, or a preference for a shorter narrative.
4. Do not reduce mature existing outcomes to a sentence, label, caption, or subordinate note when the underlying design warrants a public design section.
5. Reordering, splitting, adding, regrouping, and changing visual weight are allowed. Compression-driven information loss is not.
6. Pages may be visually restrained, but completeness may not be achieved by deleting structural layers.
7. `Technical Proof` may have controlled visual weight, but it must remain publicly visible and materially legible.
8. Cross-system rules may operate horizontally across the project, but they may not replace design chapters that must independently exist in the public project structure.
9. New content should extend or restructure the architecture first; do not remove established outcomes merely to create space.
10. Any real deletion must be recorded as `CUT` or `DEMOTE` with an explicit design reason. `Compression`, `brevity`, `page count`, `cleaner deck`, or `simpler website` are invalid reasons by themselves.
11. `CONCEPT KEEP ≠ PIXEL KEEP` remains active. Weak pixels can be redrawn without deleting the design concept.
12. `VALIDATION SUBSET ≠ WHOLE PROJECT` remains active. Evidence / technical / governance subsets cannot redefine or shrink the project universe.

The public structure remains allowed to expand beyond the current count. The architecture must preserve independently meaningful systems, including at minimum the current full C04 universe:

`Site/Journey → Culture/Content → Game Map/Route → App/Digital → Thirteen Imprints → Scene Interaction → Audience Depth → Physical/Sensory → Memory/IP → Spatial/Technical Proof → Return → Delivery/Close`

## Deletion vocabulary

If an object or chapter should actually leave MAIN, use one of:
- `DEMOTE TO SUPPORT`
- `DEMOTE TO PROCESS`
- `HOLD / FIELD OPEN`
- `CUT`

Every such decision must include a design reason: redundancy, contradiction, genericity, visual weakness after fair redraw opportunity, experience harm, or evidence/truth conflict. `Compression` is not a valid design reason.
