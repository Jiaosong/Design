## Cross-screen P0 blocking problems

Visible copy includes `Non-App Repair`, `App v1.6`, `Technical Focus v2`, `本轮不重做`, `下一轮 design delta`, and `73s Motion v1.0`. These belong in receipts/metadata, not finished public first-read pixels.

### P0-2 — Asset repetition is too high
The same R06 valley image is reused across Culture, Scene, Audience, Physical and other chapters. This collapses distinct systems and makes the project look asset-poor.

### P0-3 — Giant headline template remains over-dominant
Most sections still use the same large top-left serif headline + small paragraph + lower content block. v1.2 has structural variety in theory, but the first-read rhythm remains strongly templated.

### P0-4 — Digital and Physical do not carry independent design weight
Digital reads as screenshots. Physical reads as landscape. Both are independent C04 systems and must be publicly visible as designed experiences/objects.

### P0-5 — R13 scene proof mismatch
Current R13 does not convincingly demonstrate compression/attention narrowing. Bind the accepted R13-specific pixel or repair only the scene-integration layer around an authoritative R13 source.
