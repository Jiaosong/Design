Functional definition:

> The 1200 × 160 × 975 mm R06 object is an **interpretive / leaning / reading / short-recovery element**, not the sole fall-protection guard.

Concept-only benchmark inputs currently retained:

- direct-rock scenario: anchor size and embedment remain `UNSPECIFIED / FIELD OPEN`.

These values are design-development inputs, not structural validation.

## Material / CMF intent

- hidden primary frame: galvanized / high-performance coated structural steel, final grade by engineer;
- visible dark-copper layer: controlled coated galvanized steel preferred; weathering-steel skin remains optional pending runoff/staining review;
- touch surface: replaceable durable/modified wood;
- all critical fixings, drainage and inspection zones must remain maintainable.

## Foundation / site boundary

### Scenario A｜verified reinforced concrete
Conditional only after concrete strength, member thickness, edge distance, rebar scan, cracking condition and approved anchor system are known.

### Scenario B｜direct rock
No anchor diameter or embedment is claimed. Rock class, discontinuities, weathering and pull-out resistance require field/geotechnical/structural closure.

## Maintenance rhythm

`OPENING → MONTHLY → QUARTERLY → ANNUAL → EVENT-DRIVEN`

Failure-first principle: obvious looseness, corrosion, deformation, drainage failure or visibility/safety uncertainty triggers degrade / isolate / close before adding narrative depth.
