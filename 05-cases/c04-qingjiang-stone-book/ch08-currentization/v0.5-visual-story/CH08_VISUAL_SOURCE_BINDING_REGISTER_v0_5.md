## D｜Image-led composition constraints

- Primary visual should dominate the surface whenever it carries the claim.
- A caption may state the strategy consequence after the image; it must not pre-explain the image into irrelevance.
- Same-source paired surfaces retain `BASE_SOURCE / SOURCE_VERSION / PRESENTATION_ONLY_CHANGE / REVEAL_LAYER / DOES_NOT_PROVE`.
- Existing processed images may be recropped/resequenced/annotated; source geometry or truth status cannot be upgraded by presentation.
- Generic Qingjiang imagery, decorative landscape substitutes, or abstract gradient placeholders cannot replace a named current source at MAIN quality.
- No AI image generation is required for this reframe; any pre-existing AI-assisted conceptual visualization remains explicitly labeled as such and does not prove field conditions.
