## 3. Priority-0 node contracts

### `R05-H01` — 红花石林 / S0

Goal: the photograph itself must carry the peak-forest first reading.

- broad peak-forest contour;
- at least three readable depth layers where possible;
- vegetation-to-rock proportion remains legible;
- cabin/tourists do not become hero subjects;
- maintain 16:9 and 9:16-safe composition where possible.

Failure: generic isolated peak, excessive saturation, non-Red-Flower-Peak-Forest substitution.

### `R01-H01` — 红岩嘴 / S1

Goal: show cross-river / moving-view relation, not an isolated rock photograph.

- Qingjiang visible;
- bare-rock edge legible;
- viewpoint supports cross-river relation;
- retain negative space for observation prompt where possible.

Failure: rock-only close-up, no river, unrelated cliff substitution.

### `R06-H01/H02` — 多级阶地·不对称河谷 / S2

Goal: real view capable of supporting later 2D Reveal.

- Qingjiang main channel visible;
- both banks visible in hero where possible;
- terrace/elevation layers readable;
- preferably close to operator-described observation-platform relation;
- boat/context does not become hero.

OLEANDER authors the explanatory overlay; the photograph itself is not treated as a geology proof beyond what it visibly supports.

### `R13-H01` — 一线天 / S0

Goal: show body-scale slit and natural framing.

- upper-narrow / lower-wide slit readable;
- slit boundaries retained;
- external Qingjiang / boat / north-bank context visible where possible;
- vertical orientation preferred.

Failure: rock-texture-only image, severe distortion, unrelated gorge substitution.
