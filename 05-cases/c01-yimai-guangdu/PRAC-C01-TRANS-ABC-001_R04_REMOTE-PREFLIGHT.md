## 4. C2 comparison structure after R04

C2 is no longer framed as a visual comparison of three similar arches. The comparison sequence is locked to six layers:

1. **Object identity** — P1 / P2 / P3 remain independent objects.
2. **Direct material evidence** — record only what can actually be seen in the available photograph/site observation.
3. **Source claim** — person, date, commemorative narrative, and naming are shown as source claims, not as self-evident visual facts.
4. **Institutional question** — only after source reveal: who defined, approved, recorded, or regulated the object?
5. **Contemporary interpretation** — who narrates it now, who may revise the narrative, and whose perspective may be missing?
6. **UNKNOWN** — formal ID, protection zone, current opening, stopping safety, exact coordinate, and local acceptance stay visible when not evidenced.
