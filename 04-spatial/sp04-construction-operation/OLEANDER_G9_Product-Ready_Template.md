> `G9-ready` means a product or system has a defined structure for later operational evidence. It does **not** mean G9 has been completed or the product has been proven in operation.

## 1. Unique identity
Record the exact manufacturer, model/SKU, serial number or batch, actual production/supply entity, installation location, and approved configuration. Do not collapse brand-family evidence into a project product identity.

## 2. G8 / handover zero point
Freeze the baseline at commissioning/handover:
- measured performance relevant to the product/system;
- control configuration;
- material/filter/component state;
- photographs and calibrated test instruments;
- approved submittal and commissioning reports;
- contract/settlement boundary, warranty and transferred spares.

## 3. Operational event record
Each event should carry:
`date → running hours / use quantity → observed condition → instrument or observation evidence → cause assessment → action → part/consumable → labor → cost → downtime / impact → recurrence`.

## 4. Lifecycle-cost buckets
Keep costs separate until the comparison basis is consistent:
1. purchase and installation;
2. energy and consumables;
3. cleaning and inspection;
4. failure repair;
5. spare parts;
6. retesting / recommissioning;
7. downtime or operational impact;
8. replacement / exit cost.

Do not substitute list price or an unsourced percentage for actual lifecycle cost.

## 5. Failure triggers
Escalate the product/system when one or more of the following is evidenced:
- performance repeatedly deviates from the G8 zero point or project acceptance requirement;
- repeated faults, rising repair frequency, or unacceptable downtime;
- consumables/spares become unavailable or lead time disrupts operation;
- maintenance requires destructive access or creates new safety/compliance risk;
- actual settlement or long-term cost materially invalidates the selection assumption;
- user/operator complaints form a repeatable pattern and can be linked to observation or measured data.

## 6. Evidence rule
Without project logs, invoices/contracts, maintenance tickets, calibrated measurements, or equivalent primary records, leave values `UNKNOWN / pending project feedback`. Never invent filter replacement periods, fault rates, energy savings, maintenance prices, or settlement amounts.

## 7. First instance: DST15-250P
Current manufacturer evidence supports removable magnetic filters, removable service access to powered components, and maintenance-oriented modular design. These facts do not establish a universal replacement interval, project failure rate, or annual maintenance cost.

For the first G9-ready instance, later project feedback should capture:
- supply/exhaust airflow versus commissioning baseline;
- operating current / metered energy and running hours;
- noise and recurring complaints;
- filter state and actual cleaning/replacement events;
- faults, downtime and spare-part lead time;
- condensation/leakage events;
- actual maintenance accessibility;
- consumable, spare, labor, recommissioning and settlement costs.
