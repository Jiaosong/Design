## Official product boundary
Hilti China lists HST3 as a high-performance wedge anchor for cracked concrete, with M8–M24 options and ETA / seismic / fire document categories. Applicability is SKU- and condition-specific.

## Inputs that must be frozen before selection
1. Concrete type, design strength, cracked/uncracked assumption, member thickness and reinforcement location.
2. Design tension, shear, combined action, seismic and fire conditions; dynamic/fatigue requirements if applicable.
3. Anchor diameter/length, effective embedment, spacing, edge distance, fixture thickness, baseplate holes and group layout.
4. Corrosion environment and material version; carbon-steel HST3 and stainless HST3-R are not interchangeable durability assumptions.
5. Interface with baseplate/equipment, waterproofing, firestopping and adjacent finishes.

## Design output
- Use the applicable structural design method and current mandatory Chinese structural requirements to determine actions and safety criteria.
- Treat ETA / manufacturer data as product-performance input, not as a replacement for project structural calculation.
- Explicitly check edge distance, spacing, embedment, member thickness, concrete strength and crack condition.
- Archive approvals for the exact SKU and condition when seismic or fire performance is required.
- Drawings should state exact anchor SKU, quantity/layout, effective embedment, drilling requirements, installation/torque method, base-material assumptions, inspection requirements and no-substitution conditions.

## Installation hold points
1. Scan reinforcement / embedded services before drilling; conflicts return to design instead of field-relocating holes ad hoc.
2. Match drilling method, diameter and depth to the selected SKU and approved installation instructions.
3. Remove drilling dust/debris and inspect the hole; mechanical anchoring still requires hole-quality control.
4. Use the specified installation tool and torque / adaptive-torque process; retain tool, setting/calibration state, installer, date and anchor location.
5. Do not use forced final alignment to conceal incorrect hole or baseplate geometry.

## Inspection evidence
- Delivery identity: model, material, dimensions, packaging/batch traceability and approval documents.
- Before concealment: substrate, hole position, edge distance, spacing, embedment, installation state, torque record and photographs.
- Field pull-out / acceptance testing follows project design, JGJ 145-2013, contract and local testing requirements.
- A passing sample pull-out test does not replace design calculation or the installation-quality record for the whole area.
