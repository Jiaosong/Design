# OLEANDER Lighting Design — OPEN Domain Readiness v0.1

**Status:** `DOMAIN PROCESS OPEN / PROFESSIONAL SCOPE VERIFIED / NO CANDIDATE PROCESS YET / NO PROJECT EXERCISE / NO PROMOTION`

**Domain:** Lighting Design

**Authority position:** subordinate to OLEANDER Current Architecture Map, Professional Domain Process Contract, Cross-Disciplinary Integration, Architecture / Interior / Electrical / Controls / Emergency-Life-Safety / Daylighting / Commissioning / Operations authorities, and the responsible Lighting Design professional when appointed.

**Purpose:** define the minimum professional execution-depth floor a future Lighting Design R-E process must satisfy before OLEANDER may claim an authentic Lighting professional process.

This is deliberately **not** a Candidate process. Current repository cases do not contain a qualifying lighting project chain with photometric basis, luminaire/control documentation, implementation/commissioning readback and operations evidence.

---

## 1｜Why this readiness dossier exists

Current OLEANDER contains:
- Architecture / MEP / Landscape professional interfaces;
- spatial and visual design;
- rendering / image / visual atmosphere skills;
- product/material/CMF projects;
- design/artifact execution governance.

But repository search found no project case with a professional Lighting Design chain containing enough of:

- lighting brief / Owner’s Project Requirements;
- lighting concept tied to tasks/users/architecture;
- calculation zones / photometric model;
- luminaire schedule;
- optical/photometric data;
- daylight/electric-light coordination;
- control narrative / sequence of operation;
- emergency-lighting interface;
- circuit/driver/control compatibility;
- mounting/aiming/focusing documentation;
- energy/interface calculations;
- construction/commissioning testing;
- post-occupancy/maintenance readback.

Therefore:

`LIGHT-BASED VISUAL PROJECT ≠ LIGHTING DESIGN PROFESSIONAL PROJECT`.

## 2｜Professional source basis

Current IES Lighting Library identifies professional standards covering:
- indoor/outdoor lighting quality;
- light sources;
- luminaires;
- daylighting;
- lighting controls;
- lighting design and construction process;
- commissioning;
- sustainability;
- outdoor/environmental considerations;
- control narratives;
- maintenance;
- BIM/photometric data;
- calculations.

Current/recent examples verified in 2026 include:
- ANSI/IES LP-4-20(R2026) — electric light sources;
- ANSI/IES LP-6-25 — lighting control systems;
- ANSI/IES LP-7-20 — lighting design and construction process;
- ANSI/IES LP-8-20 — commissioning;
- ANSI/IES LP-16-22 — control intent narratives and sequences of operations;
- ANSI/IES LP-42-26 — dimming/control method designations.

IALD / CLD current competence framework also treats lighting design as a professional practice covering goals/outcomes, design solutions, documentation, collaboration and implementation guidance.

Transfer rule:

`PROFESSIONAL LIGHTING TASK EVIDENCE ≠ UNIVERSAL OLEANDER STAGE AUTHORITY`.

No IES/IALD numeric criterion becomes a project default without the applicable project/jurisdiction/source authority.

## 3｜Professional trigger

A future Lighting Design process is materially triggered when the project claims professional resolution of one or more:

- lighting concept / visual environment;
- task/ambient/accent/feature lighting;
- daylight-electric light integration;
- illuminance/luminance/glare/contrast performance;
- color appearance / color rendition;
- spectral/tunable lighting;
- luminaire/optic selection;
- lighting controls/zoning/scenes;
- dimming/protocol compatibility;
- emergency/egress lighting interface;
- exterior/site lighting;
- façade/landscape lighting;
- museum/display/conservation lighting;
- energy/code lighting interface;
- lighting commissioning;
- aiming/focusing/programming;
- lighting maintenance / replacement;
- post-occupancy lighting performance.

Not triggered by:
- render atmosphere only;
- decorative “light language” board without performance claim;
- generic fixture imagery;
- CMF/light-object concept with explicit exclusion of built-environment lighting performance.

## 8｜Professional HOLD conditions

Future professional PASS must HOLD when materially applicable and unresolved:

- calculations use stale/non-authoritative geometry;
- photometric files do not match scheduled luminaires;
- reflectance/maintenance/control assumptions are hidden;
- render image is used as photometric proof;
- luminaire schedule lacks driver/control/mounting relation;
- lighting control symbols exist without control narrative/sequence;
- emergency behavior is unresolved;
- lighting/electrical compatibility unresolved;
- ceiling/mounting/service-access conflict unresolved;
- substitution claims equivalence from wattage/CCT/appearance alone;
- daylight and electric-light controls conflict;
- glare/contrast/task outcome materially unresolved;
- exterior lighting ecology/trespass/curfew responsibility unresolved;
- commissioning criteria absent for claimed controls/performance;
- source/build/install existence is treated as commissioning PASS;
- one mockup is generalized beyond its bounded location/condition;
- maintenance/replacement path contradicts design.
