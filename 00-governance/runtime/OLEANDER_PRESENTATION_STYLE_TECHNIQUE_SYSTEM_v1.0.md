# OLEANDER Presentation Style & Technique System v1.0

Status: **DRAFT GOVERNANCE EXTENSION**. This document compiles existing OLEANDER visual/story knowledge into a project-runtime style/technique selection system. It does not replace Current Visual Communication, Visual Design, Story & Board, Image Art Direction, Motion, Data Viz, Technical Drawing or Delivery QC knowledge.

## 1. Core distinction

- **Style Profile** = a repeatable set of observable relations and behaviors.
- **Technique** = a bounded operation used to solve a communication problem.
- **Motif** = a repeated recognizable formal relation that supports identity/continuity.
- **Effect** = a local perceptual treatment; effects do not become style authority by repetition alone.
- **Reference Tag** = shorthand such as `editorial`, `cinematic`, `Swiss`, `brutalist`, `technical`, `luxury`, `playful`; useful for discovery, never sufficient as instruction.

`STYLE LABEL ≠ STYLE SYSTEM`.

## 2. Style Profile axes

Every material Style Profile should resolve the relevant axes below. Values may be qualitative, ordinal or bounded numeric where the project has an actual reason.

### A. Density
`SPARSE | OPEN | MODERATE | DENSE | ATLAS`.

Controls information load, whitespace, number of simultaneous proof objects and reading speed.

### B. Dominance distribution
`SINGLE_DOMINANT | DUAL_ANCHOR | HIERARCHICAL_CLUSTER | DISTRIBUTED | MATRIX_EQUAL`.

`MATRIX_EQUAL` is legal only when items are truly peer-level; it is not the default.

### C. Geometry / composition discipline
`ORTHOGONAL | MODULAR | AXIAL | ASYMMETRIC_GRID | FREEFORM_CONTROLLED | SPATIAL_FIELD | HYBRID`.

### D. Edge behavior
`FRAMED | INSET | BLEED | CROPPED | OVERLAPPING | BOUNDARY_CROSSING | FLOATING`.

### E. Depth model
`FLAT | LAYERED | COLLAGED | PERSPECTIVAL | SPATIAL | IMMERSIVE`.

### F. Image strategy
`NONE | DOCUMENTARY | OBJECT_HERO | LANDSCAPE_FIELD | DETAIL_MACRO | SEQUENCE | CONTACT_SHEET | ANALYTICAL_OVERLAY | CUTOUT | COLLAGE | RENDERED | SPECULATIVE_DISCLOSED`.

### G. Image treatment
`SOURCE_NATIVE | COLOR_BALANCED | MONOCHROME_SUPPORT | DUOTONE_DERIVATIVE | HIGH_KEY | LOW_KEY | GRAINED | MASKED | TONAL_UNIFIED | GHOSTED_CONTEXT | DEPTH_FADE | COMPOSITE`.

Evidence-bearing imagery defaults to `SOURCE_NATIVE` or bounded adjustments that do not change the claim. Strong stylization requires derivative labeling.

### H. Typography behavior
`NEUTRAL_INFORMATIONAL | EDITORIAL | TECHNICAL | DATA_NUMERIC | DISPLAY_EXPRESSIVE | TYPOGRAPHIC_FIELD | MONOSPACED_SYSTEM | BILINGUAL_PAIRED | MIXED_ROLE`.

### I. Contrast behavior
`LOW | RESTRAINED | STRUCTURAL | HIGH | DRAMATIC`.

Contrast describes relation, not quality. High contrast is not automatically stronger.

### J. Color strategy
`ACHROMATIC | RESTRAINED_PALETTE | IDENTITY_LED | SEMANTIC_CODED | DATA_CODED | MATERIAL_LED | EXPRESSIVE_ACCENT | FULL_SPECTRUM_CONTROLLED`.

Color cannot be the sole carrier of critical state.

### K. Shape language
`RECTILINEAR | ROUNDED | CIRCULAR | ANGULAR | ORGANIC | TOPOGRAPHIC | DIAGRAMMATIC | MIXED_CONTROLLED`.

### L. Rule visibility
`INVISIBLE_GRID | VISIBLE_RULES | HEAVY_RULES | BORDERLESS | ANNOTATION_LED | SPECIFICATION_LED`.

### M. Rhythm / pacing
`CONTINUOUS | SECTIONAL | ALTERNATING | CRESCENDO | CINEMATIC | MODULAR | BREATHER_DENSE | LOOPED`.

### N. Narrative behavior
`ARGUMENT_LED | EVIDENCE_LED | EXPERIENCE_LED | OBJECT_LED | PROCESS_LED | COMPARISON_LED | JOURNEY_LED | REFERENCE_LED | HYBRID`.

### O. Motion behavior
`STATIC | MICRO_FEEDBACK | PROGRESSIVE_REVEAL | TRANSFORMATION | CAMERA_MOVE | SCROLL_DRIVEN | STATE_DRIVEN | IMMERSIVE_MOTION`.

Motion must preserve a meaningful static/reduced-motion interpretation where applicable.

### P. Materiality / physical finish
`DIGITAL_FLAT | PAPER_EDITORIAL | TACTILE_MATERIAL | TRANSLUCENT_LAYER | FOLDED | MOUNTED_PANEL | ENVIRONMENTAL | OBJECT_INTEGRATED | MIXED_MEDIA`.

### Q. Evidence visibility
`EXPLICIT_PRIMARY | EMBEDDED_PRIMARY | SUPPORT_RAIL | APPENDIX_DEPTH | INTERACTIVE_DEPTH`.

### R. Tone outcome
Tone is recorded as an **outcome target**, not the method itself:
`CALM | AUTHORITATIVE | TECHNICAL | CIVIC | HUMAN | EXPERIMENTAL | EXPRESSIVE | PREMIUM | PLAYFUL | URGENT | REFLECTIVE | IMMERSIVE`.

A tone adjective must resolve to the observable axes above.

## 3. Style Profile archetypes

These are **candidate bundles**, not universal styles and not mandatory taxonomies. A project may combine or reject them.

### S01 Evidence-First Technical
Typical profile:
- moderate/dense;
- hierarchical cluster;
- orthogonal/modular;
- technical + data typography;
- restrained/semantic color;
- annotation/specification led;
- explicit evidence primary;
- motion minimal.

Use for: technical review, assurance, engineering proof, regulatory/coordination presentation.

Avoid: dramatic image treatment that makes precision harder to read.

### S02 Editorial Narrative
Typical profile:
- open/moderate density;
- asymmetric grid;
- editorial typography;
- image/text counterpoint;
- alternating rhythm;
- section openers + evidence spreads;
- restrained identity color.

Use for: books, portfolios, reports, long-form case studies.

### S03 Civic / Institutional
Typical profile:
- clear hierarchy;
- moderate density;
- durable typography;
- restrained identity field;
- documentary imagery;
- high source/caption visibility;
- stable navigation.

Use for: public institutions, schools, civic communication, stakeholder review.

### S04 Analytical Atlas
Typical profile:
- dense/atlas;
- matrix + hierarchical cluster;
- data/technical typography;
- semantic color coding;
- contact sheets, maps, tables, layered diagrams;
- support rails and cross-reference IDs.

Use for: research synthesis, mapping, scenario comparison, complex systems.

### S05 Hero-Led Portfolio
Typical profile:
- sparse/open;
- single dominant field;
- object/landscape hero;
- large-scale image + restrained evidence rail;
- strong page-role variation;
- high visual ownership.

Use for: project portfolio, competition boards, design reviews when strong project evidence exists.

Hard boundary: weak or synthetic hero imagery cannot outrank stronger technical/project evidence merely for drama.

### S06 Cinematic Experience
Typical profile:
- sparse/moderate;
- cinematic pacing;
- low/high-key image sequences;
- controlled crop;
- progressive reveal;
- large field changes;
- minimal on-frame text.

Use for: experience narrative, film, immersive presentation, spatial journey.

Hard boundary: cinematic continuity cannot imply false spatial continuity.

### S07 Exhibition Spatial
Typical profile:
- far/mid/near hierarchy;
- spatial-field composition;
- large-distance anchors;
- sequential panels/zones;
- physical material and lighting awareness;
- local detail depth.

Use for: exhibition walls, public installations, review spaces.

### S08 Material / Tactile Editorial
Typical profile:
- restrained density;
- material-led palette;
- texture/paper/finish hierarchy;
- close-up imagery;
- fold, transparency, layering or inserts when production justifies them.

Use for: CMF, craft, material research, premium print, object-led communication.

Hard boundary: finish cannot substitute for missing content or evidence.

### S09 Brand-Led Expressive
Typical profile:
- identity-led color/shape/type;
- expressive display role;
- controlled motif repetition;
- variable but coherent page roles;
- image treatment tied to brand authority.

Use for: brand presentations, launches, cultural projects.

Hard boundary: do not invent a brand language from presentation convenience.

### S10 Quiet Minimal
Typical profile:
- sparse/open;
- restrained contrast;
- limited roles;
- strong whitespace;
- few proof objects at larger scale;
- subtle identity cues.

Use for: high-confidence content with few strong objects.

Hard boundary: low contrast, tiny type or missing evidence does not become premium by being sparse.

### S11 Process / Workshop
Typical profile:
- moderate/dense;
- process-led narrative;
- pinned artifacts, annotations, sequence, alternatives, decisions;
- rough/working texture may be intentional but source identity remains explicit.

Use for: design development, co-design, iteration evidence, process reviews.

### S12 Data-Analytical
Typical profile:
- data-first;
- graph/table/diagram ownership;
- strong quantitative labels;
- restrained non-data decoration;
- exact source and uncertainty visibility.

Use for: performance, comparison, evidence analysis, dashboards/reports.

### S13 Immersive / Atmospheric
Typical profile:
- spatial/immersive field;
- image/sound/motion/material integration;
- low textual load in primary experience;
- depth carried by secondary/interactive layers.

Use for: exhibition, installation, spatial media.

Hard boundary: atmosphere is a presentation state, not environmental/field evidence.

### S14 Speculative Future
Typical profile:
- clearly disclosed speculative/synthetic imagery;
- strong worldbuilding;
- diagrammatic distinction between current fact and future proposal;
- scenario labels and assumption visibility.

Use for: futures, concept proposals, scenario design.

Hard boundary: speculative visual fidelity cannot upgrade feasibility or validation state.

## 4. Technique catalog

Each technique has: `technique_id, family, problem_solved, operation, suitable_evidence, risk, truth_boundary, accessibility_notes, medium_notes, readback_test`.

### C — Composition techniques

**C01 Dominant Field** — allocate perceptual area to the primary claim/proof; demote support evidence to controlled rail or secondary field.

**C02 Dual Anchor** — two materially different but co-primary proof objects held in deliberate tension/comparison.

**C03 Anchored Asymmetry** — stable alignment anchors with unequal masses; use when equal modules falsely imply equal importance.

**C04 Split Field** — divide surface into two coordinated semantic regions; useful for before/after, object/explanation, evidence/decision.

**C05 Full Bleed Evidence Field** — authoritative image/diagram controls the surface edge; text overlays only when legibility/source truth survive.

**C06 Framed Object Isolation** — isolate a single object/render/detail on a quiet field for form/material/relationship reading.

**C07 Controlled Overlap** — overlap layers to communicate hierarchy, continuity or shared territory; not for hiding source boundaries.

**C08 Negative-Space Isolation** — deliberately increase empty field around the critical object to reduce competition.

**C09 Modular Matrix** — equal/repeated cells only for genuinely comparable peers; use shared scale where comparison requires it.

**C10 Atlas / Contact Sheet** — many small related views for breadth, variation, chronology or evidence inventory; add one clear synthesis anchor.

**C11 Axial Sequence** — align content along a strong horizontal/vertical/diagonal datum to communicate progression or structural continuity.

**C12 Edge Rail** — stable narrow rail for sources, metadata, captions, legend, secondary evidence or navigation.

**C13 Layered Section Band** — repeated horizontal/vertical bands for systems, layers, time, section or operational sequence.

**C14 Panoramic Continuity** — one continuous spatial/image field across a wide carrier; only when source continuity is real.

**C15 Cropped Magnification Chain** — overall → region → detail sequence with explicit source correspondence.

### P — Pairing / comparison techniques

**P01 Before / After Lock** — same scale/view when change itself is the claim.

**P02 A / B Matched Comparison** — identical content conditions and view rules for option comparison.

**P03 Same-Source Reveal** — base image/geometry retained; overlay/highlight reveals relation without replacing source.

**P04 Diptych / Triptych** — two/three views with explicit comparative role; avoid decorative image sets.

**P05 Overview / Detail Pair** — global context and local detail shown together.

**P06 Claim / Proof Pair** — verbal claim physically adjacent to primary evidence.

**P07 Reference / Project Pair** — precedent or benchmark beside current project, with explicit `reference ≠ proof` boundary.

**P08 Positive / Negative Case** — show success and failure/contradiction to strengthen boundary understanding.

### I — Image techniques

**I01 Claim-Bound Crop** — crop to maximize the relation needed for the current claim, preserving source truth.

**I02 Documentary Full-Frame** — retain contextual evidence rather than aggressive crop when context matters.

**I03 Object Cutout** — isolate form/product/person when background is irrelevant; edge quality and grounding must survive target size.

**I04 Ghosted Context** — reduce contextual prominence while retaining enough source relation to orient the subject.

**I05 Annotation Overlay** — labels, paths, dimensions, highlights placed on source image/model without reauthoring geometry.

**I06 Masked Focus** — mask or darken non-primary regions to direct attention; keep the original available and disclose the treatment when evidence-bearing.

**I07 Tonal Unification** — bounded tonal/color correction to unify sequence; prohibited if it conceals material/state differences relevant to the claim.

**I08 Monochrome Support** — demote supporting imagery to grayscale/low chroma; never use when color itself carries evidence.

**I09 Depth Fade** — fade distant/supporting layers for hierarchy in complex diagrams or composites.

**I10 Collage / Composite** — assemble multiple source objects into one relation field; every source boundary must remain traceable.

**I11 Sequential Frames** — multiple frames show process, use, motion or time.

**I12 Macro Detail** — close crop for material/detail evidence paired with context locator when needed.

### T — Typography techniques

**T01 Claim Headline** — headline states the actual takeaway rather than a topic label.

**T02 Overscale Display Field** — typography becomes major visual mass; use when words themselves are primary identity/message.

**T03 Editorial Role Contrast** — strong display/body/caption role differentiation rather than many arbitrary sizes.

**T04 Technical Mono Layer** — monospaced/technical notation for IDs, dimensions, code or controlled metadata when it aids scanning.

**T05 Numeric Anchor** — one key number/value becomes primary, paired with unit/context/source.

**T06 Margin Note / Side Annotation** — explanatory/support material kept adjacent without interrupting main reading.

**T07 Running Label System** — stable section/object/figure labels across multi-page or exhibition systems.

**T08 Bilingual Role Pairing** — EN/ZH roles designed as semantic pairs, not identical-size duplication by default.

**T09 Pull Quote / Voice Marker** — bounded use when a real stakeholder/source voice matters; quote cannot become universal need evidence.

**T10 Typographic Index / Locator** — numbered/lettered labels that support cross-reference and navigation.

### D — Diagram / analytical techniques

**D01 Flow Chain** — sequential causality/process/decision route; arrows require actual directional meaning.

**D02 System Layer Stack** — layer decomposition with clear ownership/interfaces.

**D03 Exploded Relation** — separate parts while retaining assembly relation.

**D04 Adjacency Matrix** — pairwise relation/comparison across many items.

**D05 Trade-off Matrix** — alternatives against explicit criteria; hard constraints cannot be averaged away.

**D06 Evidence Ladder** — claims linked to source/evidence strength and validation state.

**D07 Timeline** — actual temporal sequence, version history or milestones.

**D08 Route / Journey** — spatial/service/user path with states, branches and return where relevant.

**D09 Section Strip** — continuous sectional/spatial sequence across one datum.

**D10 State Map** — interaction/service/system states and transitions.

**D11 Uncertainty Overlay** — confidence, assumption, unknown or field-open states made visible.

**D12 Dependency Graph** — upstream/downstream coupling and change propagation.

### S — Sequence techniques

**S01 Context → Tension → Insight → Proposition → System → Experience → Proof → Invitation** — existing OLEANDER narrative spine; use only where the project genuinely supports these roles.

**S02 Question → Evidence → Interpretation → Decision**.

**S03 Overview → Focus → Detail → Return**.

**S04 Before → Intervention → Consequence**.

**S05 Option A → Option B → Comparative Synthesis**.

**S06 Object → Mechanism → Performance → Boundary**.

**S07 Landscape First → Relation Reveal** using same-source gate.

**S08 Dense → Breather → Dense** pacing control when content supports pause/accumulation.

**S09 Crescendo** — gradually increase scale/intensity toward a central claim; reject if it distorts evidence priority.

**S10 Appendix Depth Routing** — remove depth from live path without deleting evidence.

### M — Motion / temporal techniques

**M01 Progressive Reveal** — reveal only when sequence aids understanding; static end state must remain meaningful.

**M02 Focus Shift** — move emphasis between existing objects without changing their meaning.

**M03 Spatial Transform** — animate correspondence between plan/section/detail/state when mapping is truthful.

**M04 Matched Morph** — transformation between comparable shapes/states with real semantic relation.

**M05 Camera Pan / Zoom** — guide attention through one large image/map/space; avoid artificial continuity.

**M06 Scroll-Driven Section Change** — interaction-driven narrative with user control and reduced-motion fallback.

**M07 State Transition** — motion explains UI/service/system state change.

**M08 Temporal Annotation** — timed labels/highlights synchronized with motion or narration.

**M09 Looping Ambient Motion** — atmosphere only; cannot be sole carrier of meaning.

### X — Interaction techniques

**X01 Compare Slider** — same-source before/after or variant comparison.

**X02 Layer Toggle** — map/system/evidence layer visibility under user control.

**X03 Filter / Facet** — explore a dataset/system by real attributes.

**X04 Hotspot Detail** — overview retains context; local detail opens on demand.

**X05 Zoom / Pan** — appropriate for maps, drawings, large images or evidence surfaces.

**X06 State Switch** — compare service/UI/system scenarios with stable labels.

**X07 Timeline Scrub** — user-controlled temporal exploration.

**X08 Progressive Depth** — summary first, evidence/details on demand.

**X09 Guided Stepper** — explicit multi-step explanation where sequence matters.

### F — Physical/material presentation techniques

**F01 Far/Mid/Near Layering** — exhibition board/wall establishes multiple reading distances.

**F02 Transparent Overlay** — tracing paper/acrylic/digital transparency for layer comparison; source alignment must be controlled.

**F03 Fold-Out / Gatefold** — reveal large map/timeline/system only when physical format justifies it.

**F04 Tab / Edge Indexing** — fast navigation in manuals/books/samples.

**F05 Material Sample Integration** — actual material/finish sample paired with specification/source.

**F06 Mounted Relief / Layered Depth** — physical depth encodes system/layer hierarchy.

**F07 Die-cut / Window** — bounded identity/navigation technique; not evidence by itself.

**F08 Foil / Emboss / Special Finish** — identity/finish technique only; cannot repair weak information hierarchy.

## 5. Technique selection matrix

### If the problem is FIRST-READ weakness
Prefer candidate operations:
`C01, C03, C08, C12, T01, T05, I01`.

### If the problem is TOO MUCH EQUAL WEIGHT
Prefer:
`C01, C03, C12, S10, T03`.

### If the problem is WEAK COMPARISON
Prefer:
`P01, P02, P04, D04, D05, X01`.

### If the problem is WEAK SOURCE/EVIDENCE RELATION
Prefer:
`P03, P06, I05, D06, D11, D12`.

### If the problem is SPATIAL COMPLEXITY
Prefer:
`P05, I05, D02, D03, D08, D09, X02, X05`.

### If the problem is SEQUENCE / PROCESS
Prefer:
`D01, D07, D10, S02-S07, M01-M08, X07-X09` as medium permits.

### If the problem is GENERIC TEMPLATE FEEL
Do not add random decoration. Reopen:
`Style Profile axes + project-specific motif + source asset role + dominant relation + page-role rhythm`.

Candidate techniques:
`C03, C07, C11, C12, C14, T02, T07, I10`, but only when tied to project logic.

### If the problem is LOW CONTENT
Prefer:
`C01, C06, C08, I03, I12, T01, T02` and fewer modules.

Do not fabricate metrics, icons, testimonials or filler modules.

### If the problem is HIGH CONTENT
Prefer:
`C09, C10, C12, D04, D06, S10, X03, X08` plus density-specific readback.

## 6. Motif contract

A motif must be described by relation, not decoration.

Record:
`motif_id, source_reason, formal_relation, allowed_surfaces, forbidden_surfaces, recurrence_rule, variation_rule, evidence_boundary, accessibility_risk, production_risk`.

Examples:
- repeated datum line derived from project section/grid;
- route ribbon derived from circulation logic;
- crop window derived from a real frame/module;
- sectional cut band repeated across boards;
- material strip derived from actual CMF system;
- recurring bilingual locator rail.

Reject motifs that exist only because “it looks cool” and have no project role.

## 8. Evidence and truth risk classes for techniques

### E0 — SEMANTICALLY NEUTRAL
Spacing, alignment, type hierarchy, non-claim-bearing background fields where no evidence meaning changes.

### E1 — BOUNDED PRESENTATION DERIVATIVE
Crop, tone adjustment, masking, annotation, comparison layout, highlight or diagrammatic simplification that preserves source meaning and is traceable.

### E2 — INTERPRETIVE DERIVATIVE
Collage, abstraction, stylized map, strong image treatment, cinematic sequencing, reconstructed diagram. Must be labeled/traceable when it could be mistaken for source evidence.

### E3 — SYNTHETIC / SPECULATIVE
Generated image, speculative render, fictional scenario or materially invented visual. Must be disclosed and cannot serve as empirical/site/product proof.

Any technique may become unsafe when it changes the audience's interpretation of the underlying evidence.

## 9. Medium-specific bundles

### Board / Exhibition
Prioritize:
`far/mid/near hierarchy, C01/C03/C12, F01, source-bound large proof, annotation legibility, physical distance readback`.

### Deck / Live Presentation
Prioritize:
`T01, P06, S02, S10, clear primary proof, playback-size readback, essential/support/appendix split`.

### Report / Book
Prioritize:
`editorial page roles, C12, T03/T06/T07, image/caption/source system, sequence contact-sheet review`.

### Web Case Study
Prioritize:
`responsive role redistribution, X04/X08, S03, motion purpose, semantic reading order, accessibility and mobile recomposition`.

### Film / Video
Prioritize:
`shot purpose, S07/S09, M05/M08, source/synthetic labeling, narration-image relation, temporal evidence integrity`.

### Technical Review
Prioritize:
`S01 Evidence-First Technical, D02/D03/D06/D12, I05, T04/T05, explicit OPEN/HOLD states`.

## 10. Hard anti-patterns

Reject as defaults:

- generic `hero + equal cards + timeline` with no project logic;
- style selection by adjective only;
- arbitrary gradient/noise/glass/shadow as identity;
- decorative diagrams that do not encode relations;
- icon walls standing in for content;
- visual hierarchy that contradicts evidence strength;
- heavy desaturation when color carries evidence;
- montage that obscures source boundaries;
- cinematic transitions that imply nonexistent spatial continuity;
- fake technical annotations;
- fake paper/material texture used to imply authenticity;
- “premium” tiny low-contrast text;
- oversize display type that displaces necessary proof;
- equal card grids for non-equal content;
- style imitation from a third-party reference without relation transfer and rights boundary;
- generative imagery used as site/product/user evidence;
- decoration added solely to fill empty space.

## 11. Validation / readback

A style or technique survives only when its actual implementation improves the current communication objective without introducing a larger truth, accessibility, identity or production regression.

Readback at the applicable conditions:

- thumbnail / first-read;
- full-frame / whole composition;
- normal reading scale;
- technical/detail scale;
- far/mid/near distance for physical display;
- responsive narrow/mobile state;
- grayscale or color-removal attack where color hierarchy is material;
- source-off / label-off tests where project recognizability matters;
- reduced-motion/static state where motion is used;
- longest-string / bilingual stress;
- print/material proof where physical finish is material.

`TECHNIQUE APPLIED ≠ TECHNIQUE WORKED`.

## 12. External calibration

This system is compatible with the current ISO 9241-112:2025 principle that information presentation is an ergonomic design problem across modalities, and with WCAG 2.2 requirements that digital information remain perceivable and not rely on color alone for critical meaning. Contemporary platform guidance also reinforces purposeful hierarchy and purposeful/optional motion. These sources calibrate constraints; they do not define OLEANDER style.
