## Main diagnosis

The system was not primarily missing design knowledge. Four closure problems were superimposed:

1. **Routing truth gap** — Skill text could describe an anchor as Current while canonical runtime said SUPPORT/REVIEW or fail-closed SUPPORT.
2. **Metadata gap** — some ACTIVE/VALID owners omitted Retrieval Space/Search Eligibility and therefore failed closed.
3. **Knowledge depth gap** — a smaller set of Current owners, most clearly Design System, lacked the back half of the 11-link reasoning chain.
4. **Practice/maturity gap** — simulated exercise, case count, page richness, CI, or producer readback must remain separate from real cross-context artifact/readback maturity.
