## Main diagnosis

The present system is not primarily missing design knowledge. Four closure problems are superimposed:

1. **Routing truth gap** — Skill text can describe an anchor as Current while the canonical runtime says SUPPORT/REVIEW or fail-closed SUPPORT.
2. **Metadata gap** — some ACTIVE/VALID owners omit Retrieval Space/Search Eligibility and therefore fail closed.
3. **Knowledge depth gap** — a smaller set of Current owners, most clearly Design System, lacks the back half of the 11-link reasoning chain.
4. **Practice/maturity gap** — simulated exercise, case count, page richness, CI, or producer readback is being kept separate from real cross-context artifact/readback maturity.
