## Locked source-bound invariants

- P02 remains a standing / half-lean / short-recovery support; both feet remain on the ground.
- Existing railing is the unverified base relation; no independent floor legs.
- Reversible attachment is required before form elaboration.
- Isolation/buffer, clamp, service fastener and body-contact surface stay separable in the product logic.
- Passage and edge safety outrank the product.
- Cleaning/drainage and replaceability remain explicit.
- C23 dimensions remain concept-envelope / design-estimate only.
- Existing rail/base load capacity, permission, required clear passage, wet/slip and safety conditions remain unverified.

## Alternatives

- **A / TWIN SADDLE PODS** — two compact independent clamp pods with replaceable liners and outward service fasteners. Lowest affected rail area and easiest local replacement; more part count and more point interfaces.
- **B / SERVICE BRIDGE** — one removable rear bridge/backplate links the contact surface to two rail interfaces and carries a continuous clean-out gap. Strongest assembly readability and fewer loose pods; broad contact zone may trap dirt/water and visually feel heavier.
- **C / SPLIT POST COLLARS** — two split collars locate on existing vertical rail posts, each supporting a short bracket to the contact surface. Clear paired service points and no continuous rear plate; highly dependent on post spacing and can project farther into the passage envelope.

These are **interface concept mechanisms**, not verified structural load paths.
