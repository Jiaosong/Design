# OLEANDER Human–AI Co-Design Architecture v0.1 — CANDIDATE

**State:** `CANDIDATE / RESTRUCTURE / NO PROMOTION`  
**Decision object:** `OLEANDER-HUMAN-AI-CODESIGN-VNEXT`  
**Base Current:** `OLEANDER_CURRENT_ARCHITECTURE_MAP_v2.1` on `origin/main@6a52a5c5`  
**Change class:** top-level operating-model restructure with no-loss preservation of existing owner-native authority.

## 1. Why this restructure exists

Current OLEANDER is strong at authority resolution, evidence integrity, professional process, execution routing, readback and persistence. Its visible top-level story, however, is still primarily:

`Authority → Runtime → Layers → Owners → Execution → Review → Promotion`.

That model is appropriate for governance and orchestration, but it under-specifies the thing the user experiences as design: a repeated human–AI loop of framing, exploring, making, seeing, critiquing, steering, developing and learning.

The vNext candidate therefore changes the visible center from **governance-first runtime** to **human–AI co-design**, while retaining governance as a continuous substrate beneath every design action.

The three views are projections over **one** Current architecture. They do not create new authority/state families. The Governance & Continuity view is always active but normally quiet in user interaction. It becomes visible when it changes what may be claimed, changed, saved, handed off or promoted.

### 3.1 Three views, one architecture

**View A — Co-Design Experience** answers: *what are the human and AI doing together right now, and what decision can change next?*

**View B — Professional & Making** answers: *what authentic discipline process, native object, tool/runtime and readback make that design claim real?*

**View C — Governance & Continuity** answers: *what is Current, what is allowed, what is stale, where can work resume, and what can be persisted/promoted?*

R-A…R-K, DD, professional stages, receipts and gates remain available in the views that need them; they are not replaced by a new vNext state model.

## 4. Human and AI are both first-class participants

### 4.1 Human owns

- project intent, values and consequential preference in context;
- tacit situated knowledge that is actually provided or observed;
- substantive design steering and rejection;
- reopening of locked design variables where authorized;
- final professional/human judgments already reserved by Current contracts;
- Candidate → Canonical / Release / Promotion decisions where Current requires human authority.

Human input is not reduced to approval. A human can frame, reframe, mix, reject, defer, ask for a wildcard, identify discomfort without explaining it, or change the value conflict being optimized.

### 4.2 AI owns, within existing authority ceilings

- active problem reframing and clarification through design consequences;
- retrieval and synthesis of applicable knowledge/evidence;
- generation of materially distinct mechanism families;
- creation of editable/native alternatives through existing execution owners;
- attack, contradiction search, simulation and comparative explanation;
- translation of human steering into explicit changed variables and second-round artifacts;
- continuity recovery across sessions from Current carriers;
- bounded explanation that helps the designer understand why alternatives differ.

AI does not infer durable taste from one selection, award itself Design KEEP, promote a project, manufacture human feedback, or convert a generated representation into field/professional truth.

## 5. Six design responsibility arenas

These are **arenas inside the three views**, not six new Runtime Layers, professional domains, Knowledge domains or state families.

### A1 — Collaboration & Steering

Purpose: keep the human and AI on the same decision object without forcing internal governance vocabulary on the human.

Responsibilities:
- natural-language intent and dissatisfaction;
- select / modify / mix / reject / reopen;
- explicit human reason capture when consequential;
- ambiguity detection before consequential mutation;
- progressive disclosure of governance only when material;
- designer-agency protection: the system proposes but does not silently decide value conflicts.

### A2 — Design Intelligence & Exploration

Purpose: convert evidence, context and intent into a real possibility space.

Responsibilities:
- frame/reframe the design question;
- map evidence / inference / assumption / unknown;
- causal hypotheses and relation synthesis;
- materially distinct option families, not cosmetic variants;
- current/no-change and deletion/OFF candidates when relevant;
- explicit falsifiers and sensitive unknowns;
- use Current Knowledge and existing Methods before inventing new methods.

Existing `R-C Design Intelligence` and the generative parts of `oleander-design-process` remain source owners for these semantics until a reviewed successor is adopted.

### B1 — Professional Design Development

Purpose: make the candidate credible inside the authentic discipline rather than inside a generic AI workflow.

Responsibilities:
- invoke the actual professional process and stage logic;
- maintain domain-native design questions and maturity;
- coordinate cross-disciplinary interfaces when coupled;
- consume applicable DD responsibilities without replacing professional stages;
- preserve statutory/technical/specialist owner boundaries.

Architecture remains Architecture; HCD remains HCD; product, service, systems, interior, landscape, lighting, structure and MEP do not become one generic `CoDesign Stage` taxonomy.

### B2 — Making, Prototyping & Simulation

Purpose: turn reasoning into something the human and AI can actually inspect.

Responsibilities:
- Required Native Output first;
- editable CAD / 3D / SVG / HTML / code / data / physical-test carriers as applicable;
- prototype fidelity matched to the unknown;
- tool/adapter selection by capability, not vendor ontology;
- simulation and state execution where the design claim depends on behavior;
- preserve one logical artifact / one Current revision / multiple representations.

The existing R-G/R-H contracts remain the execution substrate. A successful tool call is not a design verdict.

### A3/B3 — Critique, Decision & Readback

Purpose: make seeing and judging the real artifact part of design, not a downstream audit after design is supposedly finished.

Responsibilities:
- actual readback of the made object;
- whole/region/object/detail and use/state reads where applicable;
- attack and strongest contradiction;
- compare alternatives under one stated comparison world;
- human steering at consequential choice points;
- independent design/professional/technical/evidence review when triggered;
- root-cause repair followed by new readback.

This domain consumes existing R-I review owners. It does not merge their verdicts into one score.

### A4/C1 — Designer + System Learning & Evolution

Purpose: improve the artifact, the human designer's capability and OLEANDER itself without conflating those three outcomes.

Track separately:

1. **Artifact learning** — what changed in the current design and why.
2. **Designer development** — what distinction, failure mode or transfer principle the human can now recognize or apply.
3. **System evolution** — what reusable capability gap or rule has cross-context evidence strong enough to enter existing G9/Evolution routes.

Designer development is supportive, not pedagogical overhead. At useful comparison moments the AI may expose one key distinction and one transfer question; assistance should reduce when the human demonstrates stable capability.

No one project choice becomes a permanent preference profile by default.

## 6. Three nested loops

### Loop A — Co-Design Loop

```text
FRAME → EXPLORE → MAKE → LOOK → CRITIQUE → STEER → DEVELOP → repeat
```

These are **verbs**, not a new persisted state family. A project may enter the loop at any useful point.

### Loop B — Professional Development Loop

```text
DOMAIN QUESTION → NATIVE DEVELOPMENT → INTERFACE COORDINATION
→ PROFESSIONAL READBACK → REPAIR / ADVANCE / HOLD
```

This is supplied by the existing authentic professional process; vNext does not replace its stages.

### Loop C — Learning Loop

```text
OBSERVED RESULT
→ ARTIFACT LESSON / DESIGNER LESSON / SYSTEM CANDIDATE
→ bounded validation
→ project repair or existing G9/Evolution route
```

Project correction never waits for reusable-Knowledge promotion.

## 7. Governance Substrate

The following remain non-negotiable substrate capabilities and retain existing owner semantics:

- Current / Project / Source / Design Authority;
- L0–L7 Knowledge Architecture, Content Review, KI and OE;
- P0–P4 Project Axis and Current Project State;
- Control Card / Master Runtime orchestration facts;
- Execution Receipt, continuation checkpoint, concurrency and recovery;
- professional domain processes and specialist authority;
- Cross-Disciplinary Integration;

- G9 Knowledge Return and Evolution Candidate governance.

These are not removed. They stop being the primary user-facing description of what design *is*.

## 8. What changes relative to R-A … R-K

`R-A … R-K` remain a valid **implementation/runtime decomposition** during migration. They cease to be the preferred visible conceptual model for everyday human–AI design interaction.

The vNext views and design responsibility arenas are projections over existing owners, not a second execution stack. Every material write still resolves through the existing authority, owner and native-artifact paths until a separately reviewed successor contract is promoted.

## 9. Co-Design Session Kernel

The session kernel is the persistent interaction concept, but not a persistent state store.

Kernel verbs:

`UNDERSTAND → EXPLORE → MAKE → LOOK → CRITIQUE → STEER → LEARN`

Runtime support verbs:

`RESOLVE → RESUME → ROUTE → GUARD → HANDOFF → REPORT`

The first line is what makes it a design partner. The second line keeps it safe and continuous.

It consumes Current project state, Execution Receipt/checkpoint, artifact identity and persistence owners; it does not duplicate them.

## 10. Human steering contract

Consequential human feedback is captured as:

`REFERENCED OPTION/OBJECT → HUMAN ACTION → HUMAN REASON (if given) → CHANGED DESIGN VARIABLE → PRESERVED INVARIANTS → SECOND-ROUND NATIVE DELTA → READBACK`.

Valid human actions include `SELECT / MODIFY / MIX / REJECT / REOPEN / DEFER`.

If the referent is ambiguous, ask only the minimum question required to avoid writing the wrong object. If feedback is affective but non-specific (for example “不对”“太像公共建筑”), propose plausible causal interpretations as hypotheses and test alternatives; do not write them as permanent preferences.

### 10.1 Human action levels must not collapse

The system distinguishes:

`FEEDBACK_SIGNAL ≠ ITERATION_STEER ≠ DESIGN_DECISION ≠ DESIGN_KEEP ≠ PROMOTION_DECISION`.

- **FEEDBACK_SIGNAL** — perception/discomfort such as “不对”; may reopen hypotheses but does not select a solution.
- **ITERATION_STEER** — explicit select/modify/mix/reject/reopen/defer for the next design round.
- **DESIGN_DECISION** — bounded project decision persisted by the existing authorized design/project carrier.
- **DESIGN_KEEP** — independent design-quality verdict under the existing review owner.
- **PROMOTION_DECISION** — authorized Current/Canonical/Release transition under existing Promotion authority.

The plugin/AI may interpret or route the first two. It may not silently award the last three.

### 10.2 Multiple humans and decision rights

`human` is not one universal authority identity. The system may involve designer, stakeholder/client, specialist, independent reviewer and project/promotion authority. Their statements can conflict.

Use existing decision-rights/owner authority to determine what a human statement can change. The latest human message is not automatically allowed to overwrite specialist truth, independent review, project authority or another scoped owner's decision.

## 11. Default interaction principles

1. Design first, governance second in presentation order.
2. Do useful reversible work before asking for preferences that can be tested through alternatives.
3. Ask the human only for a consequential value/scope/authority choice that the system cannot legitimately decide.
4. Show actual artifacts and differences, not only explanations of what could be made.
5. Continue across ready reversible nodes; do not stop after every tool call.
6. Never hide a real HOLD, unsupported claim or authority mismatch behind a polished artifact.
7. Preserve rejected branches and the reason for consequential steering without converting one choice into a universal taste rule.

## 12. Success criteria for this restructure

The candidate is not promotable until real project trials demonstrate all of the following:

- a new user can work naturally without learning R-A…R-K vocabulary;
- “继续” resumes the verified frontier rather than chat memory;
- the system produces materially distinct editable alternatives before asking the human to choose when the design question is open;
- human mix/reject/reframe feedback causes an inspectable second-round native delta;
- authority/evidence/field/engineering boundaries remain at least as strict as Current;
- presentation derivatives cannot replace native design authority;
- no new Project State, checkpoint, artifact registry or persistence store is introduced by the session kernel;
- one spatial, one digital and one physical-product project can run through the co-design loop without forcing a universal professional stage sequence;
- a multi-human disagreement test shows that interaction feedback does not bypass owner-specific decision rights;
- designer-development support is useful but can fade without breaking execution;
- uninstalling the plugin/session UI does not destroy Current project state or artifact authority.
