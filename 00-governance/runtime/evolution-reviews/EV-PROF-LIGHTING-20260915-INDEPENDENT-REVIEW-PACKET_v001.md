## 5｜Core professional-semantic attack

### 5.1 Lighting is not lux compliance

Reject if the process can be interpreted as:

```text
illuminance target met
→ Lighting Design PASS
```

Professional Lighting must resolve people/visual tasks, space, daylight, darkness, luminance/brightness hierarchy, glare/reflection, colour/material appearance, temporal states, controls, optical/product behavior, maintenance and actual delivery/readback as applicable.

### 5.2 Lighting is not Electrical / MEP

Electrical/MEP owns electrical/service engineering within its actual scope. Lighting owns lighting professional questions and required lighting native outputs within its scope.

Reject:

```text
circuit plan exists → Lighting complete
DALI bus works → Lighting experience verified
driver powered → luminaire/space output proven
```

The process must preserve bidirectional MEP/Electrical interfaces without transferring Lighting design judgment to the interface owner or vice versa.

### 5.3 Lighting is not rendering

A visual render can communicate intent but does not prove photometric performance, field output, glare, colour appearance, installed product identity or commissioning state.

### 5.4 Darkness is a design variable

Check that the process can preserve intentional darkness, contrast, hierarchy and restraint instead of treating design quality as maximized brightness.
