# OLEANDER Independent Design Verdict Policy v1.0

Status: ACTIVE / MERGED  
Date: 2026-08-16  
Scope: ALL OLEANDER P0–P4 / Cases / Domains / design media

## Purpose

Eliminate the systematic failure mode where the same producer generates an artifact and then self-promotes it to `PIXEL KEEP`, `MAIN KEEP`, or `PROFESSIONAL FINISH PASS` because it is complete, traceable, logically correct, or better than the previous revision.

This policy operates together with:

- Existing Mature Design First
- NO COMPRESSION / NO LOSS / RESTRUCTURE WITHOUT INFORMATION LOSS
- Artifact existence ≠ Design quality
- Traceability ≠ Professional finish
- Evidence correctness ≠ Visual excellence
- Process PASS ≠ MAIN KEEP

## 1. Producer Self-KEEP Ban

In the same production cycle, the producer may assign only:

- `EXECUTED`
- `SELF-CHECKED`
- `REVIEW PENDING`
- `REVISE`
- `REJECT`

The producer may **not** self-assign:

- `PIXEL KEEP`
- `MAIN KEEP`
- `PROFESSIONAL FINISH PASS`

A producer verdict is not an independent design verdict.

## 2. Verdict Isolation

KEEP-class verdicts require a separate visual/design readback that:

1. reopens the final artifact at intended viewing size;
2. starts from the artifact itself, not the producer's explanation;
3. does not inherit producer `PASS / KEEP` claims;
4. evaluates design quality independently from execution and governance quality.

The following do **not** establish KEEP:

- file existence;
- CI/build success;
- SHA / manifest / traceability;
- source/evidence correctness;
- authority alignment;
- field-boundary correctness;
- engineering closure;
- logical completeness;
- being better than the previous revision.

## 3. Best-Existing Benchmark

Before any new artifact can receive `PIXEL KEEP` or `MAIN KEEP`, it must be compared side-by-side against the strongest Current-Authority-permitted **Existing Mature Design / Design Source**.

Minimum comparison dimensions:

- first-read;
- composition and proportion;
- visual tension;
- spatial credibility;
- material / CMF credibility;
- human scale;
- information hierarchy;
- project specificity;
- interaction meaning;
- narrative rhythm;
- cross-media consistency;
- professional finish.

If the new work is better than its previous revision but still worse than the strongest mature existing design, the valid verdict is:

- `REVISE`, or
- `REJECT`, or
- `REVERT / CURRENTIZE EXISTING`.

"Better than last version" is never sufficient for KEEP.

## 4. Verdict Separation

The following are independent states:

- `CONCEPT KEEP`
- `CONTENT KEEP`
- `TECHNICAL KEEP`
- `PIXEL KEEP`
- `MAIN KEEP`

No state automatically promotes to another.

Examples:

- a concept may be valuable while the current pixels are `REJECT`;
- technical research may be useful while remaining `SUPPORT / PROCESS`;
- evidence may be correct while the visual artifact fails completely;
- a visually competent page may still lack MAIN value.

## 5. First-Visual Veto

The first visual gate has independent veto power.

Serious failures include:

- dashboard / card-wall composition;
- research-report or student-board feel;
- methodology-card presentation replacing design;
- generic tourism template;
- generic AI atmosphere;
- weak or ambiguous first-read;
- equalized visual hierarchy;
- implausible human / material / scale reading;
- project specificity carried mainly by text;
- insufficient professional finish.

These failures require `PIXEL REJECT / REVISE` and cannot be averaged out by Evidence, Traceability, Process, Authority, Route, Field Boundary, or Engineering PASS.

## 6. Existing Mature Design First + Fair Comparison

Before production:

1. open the original mature artifact(s);
2. open the strongest current version;
3. identify their design DNA and already-solved problems.

After production:

1. reopen the new artifact;
2. compare it against those mature references;
3. reject any regression in spatial quality, visual hierarchy, product maturity, interaction quality, narrative rhythm, or professional finish.

OLEANDER is used to audit, currentize, repair, and deepen mature design. It must not replace mature design with lower-quality governance diagrams, candidate tables, technical-report aesthetics, or process dashboards.

## 7. Internal Failure Buffer

Artifacts in any of these states remain internal by default:

- `REVIEW PENDING`
- `PIXEL FAIL`
- `REVISE`
- `REJECT`
- clearly below the strongest Existing Mature Design.

Do not expose them to the user as "成果", "KEEP SET", "MAIN CANDIDATE", or equivalent unless the user explicitly asks to inspect process / failed work.

## 8. Whole-Project Readback

During continuous deepening, periodically reopen the whole mature project, not only the local object being edited.

No local line—technical detail, engineering, governance, data, visual system, interaction, or model—may consume the project's whole design identity.

This prevents local optimization from turning a complete design project into:

- a technical report;
- a methodology deck;
- a status dashboard;
- a single-object project;
- a compliance artifact.

## 9. Promotion Contract

A new artifact may enter MAIN only when all of the following are true:

1. Current Authority allows the content;
2. Existing Mature Design has been reviewed;
3. first-visual veto passes;
4. independent design readback passes;
5. Best-Existing Benchmark shows no quality regression;
6. the artifact has genuine MAIN value, not merely support/process value;
7. relevant evidence / truth / field boundaries remain correct.

Otherwise the artifact remains `REVIEW PENDING`, `SUPPORT`, `PROCESS`, `REVISE`, `REJECT`, `HOLD`, or `REVERT TO EXISTING`.

## 10. Supersession

Where older review language permits a looser interpretation of `KEEP`, this policy governs.

This policy must be read together with `OLEANDER_EXISTING_MATURE_DESIGN_FIRST_POLICY_v1.0.md` and `OLEANDER_NO_COMPRESSION_NO_LOSS_POLICY_v1.0.md`.
