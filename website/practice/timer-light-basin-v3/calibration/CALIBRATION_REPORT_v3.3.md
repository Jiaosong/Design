## 4｜Diffuser iteration chain

Diffuser was deliberately kept **REJECTED** until the actual shallow geometry became readable without deformation.

- Round 01–06: rejected; surface remained flat or lost material response.
- Round 07: geometry-height/normal response improved the cue but remained marginal.
- Round 08: controlled reflection-card direction comparison; insufficient additional volume cue.
- Round 09 B: **accepted**. Only studio-light direction/response was changed; real geometry remained unchanged. A grazing key exposes the shallow center-to-edge curvature while retaining edge thickness.

Because the modeled basin is only about 3 mm deep over a 100 mm diffuser diameter, the accepted cue is intentionally subtle. Stronger apparent curvature would misrepresent the geometry.
