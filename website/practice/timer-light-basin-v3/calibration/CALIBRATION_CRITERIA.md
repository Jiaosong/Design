# v3.3 Photography Calibration Gates

All four are hard visual gates. No final Hero / CMF render lock is permitted until all four are visually reviewed in an executable WebGL environment.

## Housing highlight
PASS requires: continuous broad highlight over the warm-grey PC+ABS candidate; curvature is readable without clipped white bands; dark flag creates an edge without making the body look metallic; parting/rim geometry remains legible.

## Diffuser volume
PASS requires: the formed concave volume and edge thickness read simultaneously; center-to-edge transition is not a flat yellow disc; emission does not clip the PMMA surface response; transmission remains subordinate to the opal body.

## Metal knob reflection
PASS requires: a controlled bright/dark reflection sweep that reads as satin/anodized metal; no brown-plastic appearance; no mirror-chrome look; cylindrical edge and side depth remain distinct.

## Contact shadow falloff
PASS requires: dense contact immediately under the foot ring, smooth penumbra widening outward, no visible rectangular shadow plane, and no floating-product gap.

Evidence boundary: the remote calibration rig is reconstructed from dimensions and radial profiles extracted from the current v3.3 GLB: Ø118 × 29 mm housing envelope, Ø100 × 6 mm formed diffuser with the measured radial top/bottom profile, 16 × 8 × 16 mm knob envelope, 112 × 2.5 mm bottom cover and 108 × 1.5 mm foot-ring envelope. It is a model-derived visual calibration rig, not the final GLB. The real GLB remains a second gate before FINAL HERO / CMF RENDER LOCK. Materials remain visualization hypotheses, not measured production data.
