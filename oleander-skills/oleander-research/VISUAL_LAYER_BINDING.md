## Research-specific visual use

- Use the existing visual-priority rule only when valid visual evidence exists: large evidence image/object before smaller evidence, visualization/relationship diagram before table, table before explanatory prose when the task supports that order.
- Do not fabricate an image, map, render, comparison, or diagram merely to satisfy image-first presentation. Missing evidence remains missing evidence.
- Visual polish must not upgrade `EVIDENCE / INFERENCE / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN` state.
- When the research question becomes a chart/map/analytical diagram, hand off to `oleander-data-viz` and its current Chart Spec.
- When research becomes a board/report/deck narrative, hand off to `oleander-story-and-board` rather than styling the evidence matrix as a final presentation inside this Skill.

## Image-processing operator routing

Use `T-VISUAL-IMAGE-OPS-001` for non-destructive crop, layer/group organization, opacity, masks, clipping, tonal/color adjustment, Smart Object/Smart Filter handling and bounded cleanup of research imagery. Preserve the unmodified source separately. Retouch, content-aware replacement, generative editing or geometry-changing transforms that alter semantic content must be labeled as derivative/conceptual and may not be promoted into evidence.

## Review inheritance

Use Artifact Review v1.1 for actual-preview checks. Research visual presentation may improve legibility and evidence access; it does not prove the underlying claim, user response, or Design PASS.
