# Oleander Image Art Direction

Improve how real photographs, scans, maps, renders, diagrams and existing visual assets carry hierarchy, atmosphere, material credibility and narrative.

## Lifecycle role

- Primary: `PRESENTATION`
- Secondary: `DESIGN`
- Status: `CANDIDATE`
- Upstream: `oleander-research`, `oleander-design-process`
- Downstream: `oleander-visual-design`, `oleander-story-and-board`, `oleander-motion`

## Required sequence

`SOURCE AUTHORITY → REAL SOURCE ASSET → IMAGE ROLE / PRIMARY CLAIM → CROP / MASK / TONAL / COMPOSITION VARIANTS → A/B → FULL-FRAME + THUMBNAIL + NEAR-READ → REPAIR → MASTER / DERIVATIVE RECORD`

## Allowed operations

- crop and reframing;
- scale and focal emphasis;
- masks and foreground/background separation;
- tone, contrast, exposure and saturation adjustments that do not alter factual meaning;
- monochrome/duotone treatments when semantically appropriate;
- diptych/triptych/contact-sheet sequencing;
- collage and multi-image relationships when they do not fabricate spatial continuity;
- image/vector/type integration;
- responsive art direction and alternate crops;
- deterministic compositing and output preparation.

## Truth boundary

1. Do not crop away claim-bearing evidence in order to make a stronger claim.
2. Do not fabricate site continuity, product condition, material state, scale or context by compositing unrelated evidence.
3. Do not use color grading to change factual material/site interpretation.
4. Generative fill, AI extension or synthetic replacement is not the default repair path and never silently becomes source evidence.
5. Preserve source identity and record derivatives. A flattened derivative does not replace the source/master.
6. When high-end beauty retouch, complex hair masking, liquify, hand-painted repair or specialist color finishing is required and no real execution owner exists, mark PARTIAL/HOLD rather than lowering the standard.

## Review

Judge primary focus, figure-ground, negative space, tonal hierarchy, crop tension, image sequence, image-text relation, material credibility, responsive crop consistency and whether the image still supports the same evidence claim after editing.

## Required output

Return source identity, operation log, A/B evidence, selected derivative, actual readback, intended claim, what changed, what was preserved, and what remains outside the current execution surface.

## Candidate boundary

This skill is Candidate and cannot self-promote. `VISUALLY STRONG ≠ SOURCE TRUE`; `DETERMINISTIC EDIT PASS ≠ PHOTOSHOP HIGH-END RETOUCH PARITY`.
