## Scope read

Reviewed `editorial-typography-systems/SKILL.md` and its detailed guide.

## Accepted delta

- define typographic roles before assigning sizes;
- stress typography with the longest real headline, control label and narrow/mobile condition;
- treat wrapping, line length, hierarchy and responsive behavior as part of the system rather than final polish;
- inspect actual rendered desktop/mobile output after implementation;
- use typography to carry hierarchy and identity rather than defaulting to one type treatment across every section.

## Rejected as universal defaults

- fixed body character-count ranges;
- fixed tracking limits;
- “software = sans + mono”, “luxury = serif” and named-product style mappings as decision rules;
- Squarespace/Vercel/Raycast/Linear-like visual identity as OLEANDER presets;
- web-only guidance as authority for print, packaging or exhibition typography.
