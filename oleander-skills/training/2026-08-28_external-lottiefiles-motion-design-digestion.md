## Material delta accepted

Independently reformulated into OLEANDER:

1. define project-specific temporal character only after Motion Role and no-motion baseline;
2. create a repeatable project-level motion grammar using a small timing palette, easing/velocity family, entrance/exit logic, continuity rule, emphasis rule and optional ambient rule;
3. choreograph multi-element responses by semantic primary/supporting/ambient ownership rather than animating everything equally;
4. use event ordering and deliberate settle/read windows to control attention;
5. treat simultaneous high-salience motion as an attention budget and stagger by reading/causal order;
6. adapt motion density and displacement by target platform/runtime instead of proportionally scaling desktop animation;
7. treat emotion as intended art direction/hypothesis, not as a proven psychological result.

## Rejected / not imported

The following external heuristics were not promoted into OLEANDER hard rules:

- fixed personality archetypes as universal defaults;
- mandatory primary + secondary + ambient layers on every animation;
- fixed `1/3` distance/concurrency thresholds as automatic Critical failures;
- universal color-to-emotion claims;
- universal duration/easing tables as truth;
- mandatory counter-motion, overshoot or Disney-principle decoration.

They may be bounded references when appropriate, but require project/runtime evidence.
