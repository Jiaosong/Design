## Material delta accepted

1. live presentation design should begin with audience, decision/message and evidence rather than presenter document order;
2. material slides should have a dominant Claim–Proof relation rather than merely a topic label;
3. shown content and spoken explanation should be separated so the slide does not become the presenter's script;
4. dense slides should be decomposed by relation before type is shrunk;
5. bullet lists should not replace unmade process/comparison/hierarchy/cause-effect structures;
6. Essential / Support / Appendix depth can shorten the live path without deleting valid evidence;
7. comparable sections benefit from parallel narrative roles even when slide counts/layouts differ;
8. progressive disclosure is a temporal presentation device and remains subordinate to evidence visibility, Reduced Motion and user-controlled navigation.
