## Material delta accepted

1. visual fidelity should not outrun functional resolution in physical-product design;
2. form selection should be tested in neutral/orthographic representations before CMF and hero presentation create commitment bias;
3. products that contain/support a payload need an explicit cavity/envelope/access relation before form lock;
4. ergonomics/human-interface questions must be bound to real scale/authority rather than inferred from AI imagery;
5. moving/transforming products need mechanism/assembly relation reasoning before persuasive render/motion;
6. design locks must state what is locked, what remains open, what evidence supports the lock and what later evidence can reopen it;
7. CMF comparison should preserve governed geometry rather than silently regenerate different forms;
8. later-phase evidence may legitimately reopen an earlier phase, with the causal change recorded.

## Rejected / not imported as defaults

- no fixed number of sketches/options;
- no mandatory phase for products that do not need it;
- no Nano Banana/image-generation dependency or prompt recipes;
- no external anthropometric or DFM numbers promoted as Current truth;
- no rule that CMF can *never* inform early form exploration; it may be recorded/considered, but visual finish cannot substitute for unresolved functional geometry;
- no user-confirmation ceremony is required at every phase when Current project authority already resolves the decision.
