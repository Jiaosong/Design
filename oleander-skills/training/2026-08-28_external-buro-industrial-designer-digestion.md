## Material delta accepted

Independently reformulated as:

`oleander-design-process/PRODUCT_FORM_AFFORDANCE_SERVICEABILITY_EXTENSION.md`.

Accepted mechanisms:

1. Maintain a form-role ledger so material features have accountable functional/interaction/structural/protective/service/identity roles.
2. Review affordance as an expected user interpretation, not merely an attractive control surface.
3. Check body/contact reality under relevant use conditions rather than a presentation pose.
4. Reject cues that falsely imply ventilation, fastening, grip, hinge, service seam or another absent function/construction.
5. Treat maintenance/disassembly/replaceability as a design question when product lifecycle makes it material.
6. Use deletion as a diagnostic test for unaccountable features, not as a universal minimalist aesthetic.

## Rejected / not transferred

- Dieter Rams / minimalist ideology as a universal OLEANDER design law;
- “instruction needed = form failed” as a universal rule;
- fixed 5th–95th percentile requirement without project/current ergonomic authority;
- fixed grip diameter, weight or repetitive-strain thresholds;
- fixed feature-removal preference over authorized expressive/brand roles;
- source output template, adversarial persona format and wording;
- any claim that repairability always outranks sealing, hygiene, safety, certification or cost.

## OLEANDER correction

Aesthetic/identity expression can be a legitimate feature role when the Current brief authorizes it. The hard failure is not “decoration exists”; it is **unaccountable expression or expression that impersonates another functional meaning**.
