## Accepted material delta

- `body approach → decision point → sightline/time → carrier → physical placement`;
- pre-decision information before route commitment;
- identification/directional/orientation/information/regulatory/landmark roles as a working decomposition, not exhaustive taxonomy;
- progressive disclosure by spatial stage;
- redundant orientation cues without color-only semantics;
- situated map check linking You Are Here, actual body orientation and physical signage labels;
- environmental identity cannot impersonate route/safety authority.

## Rejected / bounded-only

Not promoted as universal rules:
- fixed ADA mounting heights, corridor widths or contrast percentages;
- fixed cap-height/viewing-distance formulas;
- fixed visitor density or exhibit viewing distances;
- fixed sign dimensions/materials;
- success-rate claims;
- one mandatory sign taxonomy or color-zone recipe.
