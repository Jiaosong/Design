# Reality-near rules accepted

## Hard anchor first

Do not estimate before locking:
- factual dimensions;
- known objects;
- route topology;
- elevation relations;
- current works;
- official operation;
- protected status;
- existing visitor/service systems.

## Relation before precision

Known adjacency/order/topology may be usable before exact metric geometry.

## Range before point estimate

Material unknowns use a justified range/scenario rather than a visually convenient single number.

## Independent cue preference

Seek materially independent cues when reasonably possible; do not count multiple copies of one source as triangulation.

## Cross-variable closure

Examples:
- segment times ↔ official total;
- floor height ↔ stairs/levels;
- route length ↔ terrain/time;
- fixture spacing ↔ room/ceiling geometry;
- drainage direction ↔ terrain;
- local capacity ↔ system bottleneck.

## Decision tolerance

If the design remains valid across the plausible range, provisional continuation may be justified.

If the design works only at one unverified point, revise or HOLD.

## Reversible under uncertainty

Prefer:
- relocatable;
- adjustable;
- modular;
- no-contact;
- low-impact;
- scenario-robust design
when reality is not yet closed.
