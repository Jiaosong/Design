## Accepted delta

- wayfinding as four distinct cognitive duties: Orientation → Route Decision → Route Monitoring → Destination Recognition;
- distinguish underlying IA quality from wayfinding cues layered onto that IA;
- design recovery for wrong turns and lost state;
- search as recovery/escape, not a substitute for coherent navigation;
- physical concepts such as paths, edges, zones/districts, nodes and landmarks can be useful reasoning models when the analogy is explicit;
- repeated orientation cues, consistent destination naming and arrival recognition are load-bearing rather than decorative.

## Rejected as universal defaults

- fixed 5-second/60-second heuristic thresholds;
- automatic translation of physical wayfinding metaphors into software or vice versa;
- color-zone, breadcrumb, landmark or sign patterns as mandatory solutions;
- research-literature references as direct evidence that a specific project performs well;
- copied examples/prose from the external Skill.

## Evidence boundary

Physical/spatial wayfinding remains subject to real geometry, sightline, accessibility, signage, field and behavioral evidence. A software analogy or design principle does not establish field performance. The transferable output is the independently reformulated relationship model, not the external wording or worked examples.
