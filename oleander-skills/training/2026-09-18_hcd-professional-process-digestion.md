# Professional source verification｜2026-09-18

## ISO 9241-210:2019

Verified current ISO page:
- https://www.iso.org/standard/77520.html

The page states Edition 2 / 2019 was reviewed and confirmed in 2025 and remains current.

Accepted transfer:
- human-centred design applies across the life cycle of interactive systems;
- context/user evidence, design activity and evaluation must remain part of the professional process rather than a one-time UI phase.

Rejected transfer:
- no ISO stage numbering is imported into OLEANDER;
- this source does not grant accessibility/security/privacy/live-product PASS.

## ISO 9241-11:2018

Verified current ISO page:
- https://www.iso.org/standard/63500.html

The page states the publication was reviewed and confirmed in 2023 and remains current.

Accepted transfer:
- usability is an outcome of actual use in a specified context;
- users, goals/tasks, resources and environment matter to the claim;
- effectiveness, efficiency and satisfaction cannot be inferred from interface polish.

Rejected:
- no claim that one method/test universally proves usability.

## ISO 9241-115:2024

Verified current ISO page:
- https://www.iso.org/standard/80773.html

The standard explicitly covers guidance for:
- conceptual design;
- user-system interaction design;
- user-interface design;
- navigation design.

Accepted transfer:
- HCD professional work must distinguish conceptual/interaction/navigation design from visual UI styling.

## W3C WCAG 2.2

Verified:
- https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/

Current published W3C Recommendation.

Accepted transfer:
- accessibility technical requirements and evaluation evidence are material when web/digital accessibility is claimed.

Rejected:
- `ACCESSIBLE-LOOKING` or design intention cannot be called WCAG conformance;
- OLEANDER machine validation does not award a conformance level.

Verified current public guidance:

- https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/plan-user-research-for-your-service
