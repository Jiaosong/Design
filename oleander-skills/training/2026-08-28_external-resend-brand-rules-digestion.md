## Scope read

## Accepted high-level delta

Only independently worded operational patterns were retained:

- a brand manual becomes more usable when rules are explicit rather than adjective-only;
- distinguish asset/role, allowed use, forbidden use and context-dependent exceptions;
- show operational rules through specimens/examples at the point of use;
- connect logo/type/color/image/layout rules to stable source/asset identities;
- test brand rules across more than one realistic medium before calling them reusable;
- keep semantic color roles distinct from decorative color use when the brand requires that separation.
