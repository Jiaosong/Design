## What C04 already proves

At Design Intelligence / bounded remote-analysis level:
- river / shore / peak / valley relations;
- boat / cable / walk viewpoint relations;
- route as network;
- open / compressed spatial sequence;
- relative body-attention and content-capacity consequences;
- regional generalized terrain/slope/aspect;
- potential D8 terrain-convergence pattern;
- relative solar scenarios.

## What it does not prove

- surveyed site boundary / exact topography / measured levels;
- professional grading design;
- catchment / outfall / drainage / stormwater hydraulic performance;
- soil profile / compaction / contamination / infiltration;
- professional vegetation/tree inventory or condition;
- site ecology/habitat condition;
- plant suitability / root-volume / mature performance;
- irrigation source / quality / demand / zoning;
- professional landscape maintenance / establishment capacity;
- Landscape professional PASS.

Current C04 evidence itself states:
- `REGIONAL DATA ≠ SITE OBSERVATION`;
- `REMOTE TERRAIN ≠ FIELD MEASURED`;
- `ROUTE TOPOLOGY ≠ SURVEY GEOMETRY`;
- `DERIVED DRAINAGE ≠ HYDRAULIC DESIGN`;
- `FIELD_OBSERVED=0 / FIELD_MEASURED=0 / G1F HOLD`.

The Candidate process preserves those boundaries instead of filling the missing professional fields with inference.
