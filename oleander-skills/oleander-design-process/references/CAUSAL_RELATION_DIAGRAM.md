# Causal Relation Diagram — Candidate Practice Gate

Status: PRACTICE_EVIDENCE / NOT ACTIVE / NOT PROJECT AUTHORITY

Use when a design analysis claims a causal chain such as `Evidence → Finding → Design Consequence` and a card/grid layout risks flattening the relation into peer categories.

## Rule

When the design question is causal, visual mass must encode causal direction. The Design Consequence must modify the actual spatial/product/system representation, not exist only as a final strategy card.

Do not default to equal-card grammar for evidence, inference, assumption and decision. Equal cards encode category membership and peer status; they do not by themselves prove why a design changed.

## Required sequence

`OBSERVED RELATION → INFERENCE → ADVERSE CONDITION / ASSUMPTION → DESIGN CONSEQUENCE → ACTUAL DESIGN CHANGE`

Keep `E / I / A / D` semantically distinct. If an assumption is deleted or weakened, the design consequence must either remain a reversible test or be withdrawn; it must not silently become fact.

## Required attacks

1. `TITLE-OFF` — remove headings. Causal direction should remain visible.
2. `STRATEGY-PROSE-OFF` — remove the final explanatory sentence. The actual design change should remain visible in the represented object/system.
3. `ASSUMPTION-DELETE` — remove the adverse-condition assumption. Check whether the consequence still has legitimate evidence or must fall back to a reversible test.
4. `CARD-SWAP` — try swapping the final decision with an unrelated strategy. If the graphic remains equally plausible, the diagram is not causally bound.
5. `EQUAL-CARD ATTACK` — rebuild the same content as peer cards. Use the failure to verify what causal/spatial information is lost.

## Counterexample

Equal cards are valid for non-causal inventories, peer comparisons, taxonomy, option matrices or independent evidence classes. Do not force a causal spine when no causal claim exists.

## Promotion test

`DELETE TITLES + STRATEGY PROSE → CAN A REVIEWER STILL SEE WHAT RELATION WAS OBSERVED AND EXACTLY WHAT CHANGED IN THE DESIGN?`

## Non-proof

This gate does not prove field observation, causal truth, crowd flow, service capacity, engineering validity, final presentation quality or project approval. Current maturity: `PRACTICE_EVIDENCE`. Next missing evidence: a materially different cross-context test.
