## Consolidated knowledge now supported by bounded practice

1. **Representation** — source controls, evaluator, evaluated sampling and evaluated surface are separate authorities.
2. **Dependency** — timeline, modifier stack, node graph, solver and declarative rebuild are different edit/evaluation semantics.
3. **Instances** — prototype/reference + transform/attributes is not realized mesh geometry.
4. **Surface detail** — geometry/displacement/normal/bump choice depends on `frequency × view × required cue`.
5. **Material data** — identical texture bytes do not imply identical shader-input semantics.
6. **Interchange** — units/axis/UV/tangent/handedness/transform determinant/texture pixel/fragment output form a dependent handoff contract.
7. **Operators** — kernel return/API validity and requested parameter values do not prove realized design intent; semantic success requires declared postconditions and failure sweeps.
8. **Review** — native/runtime evidence can PASS while Artifact Review or Design Review remains FAIL.
