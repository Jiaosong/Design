## Failure knowledge

1. `Hue contrast ≠ action semantics.` A brighter or different-colored sleeve can make a part noticeable without making its action discoverable.
2. `Part distinction ≠ direction cue.` A stronger value boundary may separate components but still leave slide/rotate/press/release ambiguous.
3. `Render polish can camouflage ambiguity.` Controlled highlights and premium finish may make an unresolved mechanism look intentional.
4. Diagnostic exploded/reveal views cannot be used as evidence of normal-use discoverability.
5. Color, marking and texture must be removed in sequence during review; if the action collapses, the earlier cue layer is incomplete.

Reusable for XJ01 sleeve/head release work, cleaning-tool grips, switches, latches, sliders, knobs, closures, assembly interfaces, wearables, appliance controls and any 3D/CMF study where surface treatment is expected to communicate manipulation.
