# Evidence register

## Verified principles
- U.S. DOE Building Science Education — **Build Sill Pan: Fully Flashed Window and Door Openings**: window/door openings interrupt the drainage plane; sill flashing drains out; side flashing overlaps sill flashing; head flashing overlaps side flashing; integrate flashing with the wall drainage layer.
- U.S. DOE Building Science Education — **Rough Opening Flashing: Air Sealing**: rough-opening gaps require an air seal; low-expansion foam / caulk / backer rod are possible approaches; fibrous insulation alone is not an air seal.
- Building Science Corporation — **BSI-085: Continuous Insulation and Punched Openings**: wall and window water/air/thermal control layers must connect; inset windows use a sloping sill, pan flashing, jamb/head flashing and interior perimeter air seal.
- Schüco official public information — **AWS 75.SI+**: 75 mm basic depth is publicly documented. R08C uses only that depth as a dimensional reference envelope and does not reproduce proprietary profile geometry.

## Not verified / intentionally pending
- exact frame profile, flange/rebate geometry and drainage chambers;
- anchor type and spacing;
- selected WRB/liquid flashing/tape/sealant compatibility;
- cladding subframe and firestop;
- thermal-bridge ψ value, fRsi and condensation check;
- local code, project climate and wind-driven-rain exposure;
- water penetration test pressure and acceptance criteria.
