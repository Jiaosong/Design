## Learning object
Louis I. Kahn, Salk Institute, La Jolla. Official Salk material describes two mirror-image structures flanking a grand travertine courtyard. Getty Conservation Institute independently records two nearly identical wings mirroring each other across a paved central plaza.

Sources:
- https://www.salk.edu/about/about-salk-architecture/
- https://www.getty.edu/projects/salk-institute-conservation/louis-i-kahn-designing-salk-institute/

## Visible Fact
The courtyard is not merely leftover space between buildings. Large flanking masses establish the edges, while the narrow central water channel gives the open field a strong directional center toward the Pacific.

## Design Inference
A powerful open field is actively protected by edge, direction and center relationships. In visual communication, annotations may frame a primary image, but should not occupy its primary recognition field.

## Candidate rule
`PRIMARY CLAIM → DEFINE PROTECTED RECOGNITION FIELD → PLACE RELATION AT EDGES → SHORT POINTER / BRIDGE → NEAR-READ PROOF`

### Problem
A layout may have substantial whitespace yet still interrupt the primary image or spatial read with floating explanation blocks.

### Trigger
A landscape, render, map, product or primary visual must remain the first-read while explanatory relations and proof stay visible.

### Inputs
Approved primary visual, approved support information, target viewing distance, source/truth boundaries.

### Visible Symptoms
Primary contour or directional field is repeatedly cut by labels/cards; at thumbnail scale, overlays read before the primary visual.

### Cause
Whitespace is treated as an aesthetic remainder rather than a protected recognition zone.

### Technique
Define the non-occupiable recognition field before placing annotation. Move explanation to edges and connect with short bridges/pointers. Preserve all information.

### Parameters / Conditions
The protected field is relational, not a fixed percentage. It must preserve the primary contour, directional cue and first-read at the target viewing distance.

### Aesthetic Judgment
Open space is successful only when it is structurally legible and actively protects the primary read.

### Verification
Thumbnail/distance first-read + full-resolution near-read. The primary visual must remain first, while support content remains recoverable.

### Failure Condition
A page appears clean and spacious, but labels, cards, legends or nodes still interrupt the main contour/direction. This should REVISE even if total whitespace is high.

### Counterexample
Deleting necessary proof to manufacture empty space is not PASS. `NO COMPRESSION / NO LOSS` remains mandatory.

### Transfer Boundary
Do not copy Salk geometry as a project form. Do not convert a visual protected field into invented site geometry. Do not hide required safety/route/proof information when it has higher decision priority.
