# OLEANDER AI Design Evaluation Standard v0.1

Date: 2026-08-06

## Status
`PROVISIONAL GOVERNANCE STANDARD / TESTED ON TWO CROSS-MEDIA PROTOTYPES`

## Evidence boundary
All dimensions, thresholds, weights, ratings and geometry in this package are exercise-only hypothetical parameters or designer judgments unless explicitly identified as computed evidence. They do not represent a real site, building, identity-recognition study, code review, engineering result, user test, or implemented performance.

## Principle
AI may organize evidence, identify contradictions, calculate declared metrics and compare variants. AI must not invent missing evidence, convert designer preference into fact, or promote a hypothesis into a validated rule.

## Mandatory evidence gate
An evaluation is `BLOCKED` when:
- evidence references are missing;
- exercise parameters are presented as real project data;
- unknowns are omitted;
- the artifact is called implemented, verified or successful without matching evidence;
- critical criteria are assigned scores despite `UNKNOWN` evidence.

## Weighted criteria
| Criterion | Weight | Question |
|---|---:|---|
| Relation clarity | 20 | What relation is reorganized, and is it readable? |
| Hierarchy | 15 | What is read first, second and later? |
| Rhythm | 10 | How do repetition, pause and change operate? |
| Geometry / proportion / scale | 15 | Are boundaries and dimensions internally coherent? |
| Traceability / reversibility | 20 | Can each output be traced to a declared rule and input? |
| Cross-media transfer | 10 | Does the same relation survive another medium without becoming decoration? |
| Reality evidence | 10 | What has been tested in real context, production or use? |

Weights are a v0.1 governance choice, not empirical truth. They remain open to revision after monthly reviews.

## Rating scale
- 0 — No evidence or contradiction.
- 1 — Claim stated; evidence insufficient.
- 2 — One prototype or partial evidence.
- 3 — Multiple consistent tests with limitations recorded.
- 4 — Independent or real-context verification with traceable evidence.

## Decision rules
- `BLOCKED`: evidence gate fails; no weighted decision.
- `< 50`: reject or reconstruct.
- `50–69`: iterate.
- `70–84`: provisional candidate within the stated scope.
- `85–100`: validated only for the explicitly documented scope; this does not imply universal quality or market success.
- Any critical unknown can override the weighted score.

## Required AI output structure
1. Evidence inventory.
2. Facts and computed results.
3. Designer judgments.
4. Hypotheses.
5. Unknowns and blockers.
6. Criterion-by-criterion ratings with evidence references.
7. Decision limited to the tested scope.
8. Rules that may or may not be upgraded.
