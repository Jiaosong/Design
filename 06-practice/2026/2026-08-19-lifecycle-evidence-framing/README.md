## Failure knowledge

1. **Macro detail ≠ lifecycle severity.** A tiny watermark or scratch can be visually dramatic at macro scale while having little whole-product consequence.
2. **Same state name ≠ same carrier requirement.** WET may ask a whole-product continuity question; residue may ask a lower-sleeve question; ageing may ask an interface question.
3. **Different crop/exposure inside a baseline pair is a hidden variable.** It can manufacture or suppress apparent failure.
4. **More detail can reduce truth.** Orphan macro crops remove location, adjacency and whole-product consequence.
5. **Neutral comparison is not hero rendering.** Dramatic grazing/highlight can diagnose a surface, but must not become the only carrier for a severity claim.

## Effective repair

Use `CLAIM → MINIMUM SUFFICIENT CARRIER → LOCKED BASELINE PAIR → WHOLE CONSEQUENCE WHEN REQUIRED`.

Failure-seeking readbacks:

`BASELINE_PAIR / WHOLE_CONSEQUENCE / CROP_RETURN / SEVERITY_LOCK / CLAIM_CARRIER / NEUTRAL_LIGHT`.

## Cross-project transfer

Applicable to:

- XJ01 wet / residue / dirty-wiped / scratched / aged CMF evidence;
- other OLEANDER product CMF and durability proxy renders;
- architectural/landscape material weathering studies when local damage is compared with whole-surface consequence;
- product finish, coating and cleaning-result comparisons;
- any before/after render set where framing can bias perceived severity.

Not directly applicable to:

- hero renders whose purpose is presentation rather than evidence;
- microscopy/material-science imagery where the decision claim is explicitly microscopic and scale-calibrated;
- physical lab evidence with a mandated test-camera protocol, which has higher authority;
- genuinely different product geometries or conditions where matched framing would conceal the real experimental difference.
