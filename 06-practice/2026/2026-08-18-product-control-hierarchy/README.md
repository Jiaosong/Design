## Increment over v0.1 candidate
The previous rule established that area, repetition, boundary and whitespace should encode task hierarchy. This round adds a second control variable:

`CONTROL PROMINENCE = task frequency + reach immediacy − accidental-trigger tolerance`

A frequent low-error-cost action may occupy a larger contiguous surface. A high-error-cost or infrequent lock should be smaller, recessed and spatially separated rather than visually equal.

## Ergonomic evidence boundary
- PubMed PMID 14605652 reports a modeled optimum cylindrical tool-handle diameter of about 33 mm for the general population.
- Additional ergonomic literature commonly places power-grip single-handle diameters around 30–50 mm depending on task and population.
- Exercise target shown in the SVG: handle Ø 30–38 mm and grip contact length 120–150 mm. These are **recommended study values**, not Baojiajie measured facts.
- FIELD / prototype checks remain required for hand-size coverage, wet friction, force, trigger travel, lock safety, glove use and fatigue.
