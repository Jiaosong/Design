# OLEANDER Practice — Endorsement Hierarchy

## Training ID
`OLEANDER-TRN-2026-08-17-ENDORSEMENT-HIERARCHY`

## Trigger
Current C04 Qingjiang Stone Book brand rebuild defines:

- `恩施大清江 / ENSHI DAQINGJIANG` = scenic/operator endorsement;
- `清江石书 / QINGJIANG STONE BOOK` = experience identity.

The current reusable Story/Board Skill did not yet prevent an endorsement from becoming an accidental second hero.

## Existing-first

Reused `oleander-story-and-board` rather than creating a new brand framework. The practice changes only hierarchy and role expression; it does not redraw the locked/legacy Qingjiang logo and does not claim a 1:1 reconstruction.

## Actual practice

One editable vector lockup calibration was produced through three review states:

- **v1 — REVISE:** operator endorsement and experience identity use near-equal scale/weight and compete for first-read.
- **v2 — REVISE:** first-read hierarchy works, but CJK glyph fallback in the application strip produces missing boxes; export success does not override this visual defect.
- **v3 — KEEP FOR TRAINING:** experience identity clearly owns first-read; endorsement stays legible; CJK application-strip rendering is repaired; a 480×300 distance-read derivative confirms the hierarchy survives reduction.

## Extracted rule

`ROLE CONTRACT → LOCKUP HIERARCHY → DISTANCE READ → NEAR READ → CROSS-APPLICATION READ → AUTHORITY / LEGAL BOUNDARY`

An endorsement is not automatically a second hero. When an experience/product identity owns the proposition, keep the endorsement visible but subordinate unless the brief explicitly declares equal co-brand status.

## Failure knowledge

1. Equal or near-equal logo scale can silently rewrite brand architecture even when the written strategy is correct.
2. Hiding required endorsement information is not a valid hierarchy fix.
3. Export success does not prove typography integrity; a missing-glyph fallback is a Design REVISE.
4. A ratio that works only on one hero lockup is not yet a stable system; cross-application reading is required.
5. Visual hierarchy does not establish trademark rights, operator approval, legal permission, or final brand governance.

## Transfer

Applicable to destination/operator + experience brands, parent/sub-brand systems, institutional endorsement, sponsor endorsement, product-family endorsement, packaging, signage, digital hero, publication, exhibition boards, and service/return surfaces.

Not applicable when the brief explicitly requires equal partnership; declare `CO-BRAND` and review balanced dual ownership instead.

## Truth boundary

`CALIBRATION ONLY / NO LEGACY LOGO TRACE / NO TRADEMARK OR LEGAL APPROVAL / NO PROJECT BRAND DESIGN PASS`.

Artifact existence, GitHub sync, CI, or readback do not promote the C04 brand rebuild or this rule beyond its stated training evidence.
