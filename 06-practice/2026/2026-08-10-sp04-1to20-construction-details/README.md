## Control-layer logic grounded in external building-science guidance
- opening flashing must integrate with the wall drainage/water-control plane;
- sill pan drains outward;
- jamb flashing laps over sill flashing;
- head flashing laps over jamb flashing;
- rough-opening perimeter receives a dedicated interior air seal;
- fibrous insulation alone is not treated as an air seal.

## Prototype-specific construction idea
### A — Deep Reveal
Generic 75 mm thermally-broken frame positioned 150 mm from the exterior face, leaving a deep interior reveal. A 30 mm insulation return and exterior-aligned water-control interface reduce exposure of the structural edge. The inhabited sill remains conceptually retained.

### C — Offset Frame
Frame plane is 270 mm from exterior / 90 mm from interior. The wall water-control plane lies farther outboard, so a waterproofed rough-opening liner bridges from the air/water layer to the inset frame. This bridge is intentionally visible in the 1:20 detail and is the key unresolved interface to manufacturer/system selection.

### D — Opening Field
Typical repeated frame is close to the control-layer plane. The 1:20 jamb/pier plan treats the repeated pier as the governing module rather than detailing each window independently.
