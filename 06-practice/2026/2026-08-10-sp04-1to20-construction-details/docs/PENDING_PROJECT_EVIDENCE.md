# Pending Project Evidence

Before any R08 detail becomes a project detail, confirm:

- project city, climate and exposure;
- wall structural system and actual core thickness;
- local energy / fire / acoustic / waterproofing requirements;
- actual frame manufacturer and series;
- frame installation zone and anchor calculations;
- selected WRB / air barrier / sill pan / flashing compatibility;
- insulation product and reveal-board product;
- exterior cladding support and cavity fire-stopping;
- sill metal / stone specification and drainage outlets;
- head/jamb end dams where required by the chosen system;
- thermal-bridge and surface-temperature analysis;
- condensation risk;
- water penetration testing requirement / mock-up;
- tolerance stack and construction sequence.

Current drawings are controlled prototypes, not issued-for-construction documents.
