# Control Layer Logic

## Water
The opening interrupts the wall drainage plane. The R08 details therefore draw a continuous path from wall air/water-control layer into sill/jamb/head flashing and back outward.

Sequence represented:
`sill pan → jamb flashing over sill → head flashing over jamb → drainage to exterior`

## Air
A separate interior perimeter air-seal line is drawn between frame and rough opening. This is intentionally distinct from cavity insulation.

## Thermal
The exterior continuous insulation turns into the reveal as a 30 mm training return. This is a geometric strategy only; no psi-value or condensation calculation has yet been run.

## Frame
A generic 75 mm thermally broken frame is used only to force realistic spatial relationships. No manufacturer has been specified.

## Finish / closure
Exterior sill flashing includes an outward slope and drip. Interior closure returns to the frame without crossing or blocking the exterior drainage path.

## Key unresolved issue
Prototype C has the longest distance between the main wall water-control plane and the frame plane. It therefore requires the most explicit waterproofed rough-opening liner and should be the first prototype to receive manufacturer/system validation.
