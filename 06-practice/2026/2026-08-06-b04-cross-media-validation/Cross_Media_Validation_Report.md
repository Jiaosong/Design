## 3. AI Design Evaluation Standard

**Decision:** VALIDATED FOR INTERNAL PROVISIONAL USE.

The standard can prevent unsupported claims and organize evidence, but its weights are governance choices rather than empirical truth. It must remain versioned and be calibrated through weekly and monthly reviews.

### Mandatory rule
AI may calculate declared metrics and compare variants, but may not invent missing evidence or convert designer judgment into fact. Missing critical evidence blocks rule promotion even when a weighted score is high.

## Rule upgrade status

### Upgrade
- One relation model may be translated across media when inputs, transformations and outputs remain traceable.
- Cross-media success must be judged separately for each medium; visual similarity alone is insufficient.
- Evidence gates override weighted scores.

### Do not upgrade
- The relational field is the final OLEANDER Logo.
- The generated architecture is feasible or compliant.
- A score above 70 proves design quality or public success.
