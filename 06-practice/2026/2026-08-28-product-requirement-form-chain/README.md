# Product Requirement → Form Chain

Status: CROSS_CONTEXT_EVIDENCE CANDIDATE / NO PROMOTION

GAP → Prior causal-relation practice proved evidence→consequence in route/service diagrams, but transfer to physical product form was unproven.

SOURCE → Existing `oleander-design-process` Candidate, especially product need→function→component→proportion→form→structure→material/interaction and Semantic-dimension-to-form gate.

ARTIFACT → `OLEANDER_PRODUCT_REQUIREMENT_FORM_CHAIN_R01.svg` compares three synthetic hand-tool interaction forms for a wet-glove axial-slide requirement.

A/B/ATTACK → A finish-first color ring; B axial sleeve with travel/stop/grip geometry; C notched collar. Tests: COLOR-OFF, ROTATE/DIRECTION ATTACK, deletion of labels/color, adverse wet-glove condition.

READBACK → A loses part role when color is removed and remains rotation-ambiguous. B preserves part role, direction, travel and stops without color/text. C preserves discoverability but not axial direction.

FAILURE / ROOT CAUSE → Visual salience and geometric difference were being mistaken for interaction semantics. Finish can amplify a form proof but cannot substitute for requirement→part-role→interaction causality.

REPAIR / RETEST → Select B as the stronger design-process mapping; keep CMF downstream. Retest after deleting color/text: axial travel remains legible. Physical force, grip, tolerance and wet friction remain VALIDATION HOLD.

TRANSFER RULE → For physical-product reasoning, require a visible chain `requirement → function → part role → proportion → form/structure → interaction`; delete finish and labels before crediting form proof.

BOUNDARY → Not sufficient for ergonomics, mechanism, manufacturing, wet-friction, safety or engineering approval. Decorative/non-interactive parts do not require interaction semantics.

STATUS → `CROSS_CONTEXT_EVIDENCE` for the bounded causal-form rule only; `oleander-design-process` remains CANDIDATE and is not ACTIVE.
