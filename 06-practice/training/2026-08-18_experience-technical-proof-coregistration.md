## Why this training exists

Current C04 material freezes the R06 experience carrier: technical proof may clarify how the spatial event works, but may not redesign the experience after the fact. Recent training already covered same-source paired view, exploration motion grammar, world-viewport framing and small-multiple comparability, so this round does not repeat those methods.

The uncovered problem is cross-representation fidelity: an experience/perspective carrier and a technical/section carrier can both be individually clear while silently describing different spatial events.

## Training question

How can a technical proof change representation without changing the defining spatial relationship of the experience it claims to prove?

## Actual exercise

Created a 1920×1080 editable vector study using synthetic geometry only. It pairs:

- A / EXPERIENCE CARRIER: landscape-first perspective with a subordinate platform and three spatial anchors.
- B / TECHNICAL PROOF CARRIER: section using the same relation but a different representation.

Anchor semantics:

- `A = path edge`
- `B = body position`
- `C = outer platform edge`

Relations to preserve:

- `A→B = approach-to-body`
- `B→C = body-to-edge`
- `A→C = total support`

A deliberately rejected variant moves B outside the A–C span in the technical carrier. The section becomes graphically easy to explain but no longer proves the same spatial event.

Truth boundary: `synthetic geometry / NTS / FIELD OPEN / training-only`. The study does not prove C04 site dimensions, structural adequacy, accessibility, field geometry, constructability or Design PASS.

## Failure knowledge

1. A technical drawing can be visually clear and still be false to the experience carrier.
2. Moving, mirroring, widening, flattening or inventing a defining anchor “for clarity” is redesign, not proof.
3. Object identity alone is insufficient. Preserve relational invariants: ordering, sidedness, containment, adjacency, approach-to-body and body-to-edge.
4. Correct labels or dimensions cannot rescue a carrier whose primary spatial relation has drifted.
5. Schematic co-registration does not imply field, structural, accessibility or constructability authority.
6. Producer self-review is not independent Professional Design review.
